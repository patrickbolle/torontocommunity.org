# 📖 Zine Making & Risograph in Toronto

## Toronto Zine Library
- **What:** A volunteer-run library and archive of zines tucked onto the Tranzac's second floor. Pull up a chair, read through decades of DIY publishing, and talk shop with the zinesters who keep the shelves stocked. The collective also runs bookbinding and zine-making workshops through the year
- **Where:** Tranzac Club, 292 Brunswick Ave (second floor)
- **When:** Tuesdays & Fridays 6–8pm
- **Cost:** Free
- **Find it:** [torontozinelibrary.org](https://www.torontozinelibrary.org/)

## Paperjam
- **What:** The Toronto Zine Library's annual zine fair, now two decades old — an afternoon of small-press tables, zine swaps, and the city's DIY publishing crowd taking over the Tranzac. Recent editions have filled the whole club to mark the library's 20th anniversary
- **Where:** Tranzac Club, 292 Brunswick Ave
- **When:** Annually in late March
- **Find it:** [torontozinelibrary.org/paperjam-2026](https://www.torontozinelibrary.org/paperjam-2026/)

## Zine Dream
- **What:** Toronto's long-running annual small-press art fair, with dozens of exhibitors selling zines, comics, prints, and artist books. A relaxed, hands-on room where you can meet the makers, trade your own work, and find the titles that never make it into bookstores
- **Where:** Parkdale Hall, 1605 Queen St W
- **When:** Annually in August
- **Find it:** [zinedream.com](https://www.zinedream.com/)

## Colour Code
- **What:** Independent risograph print studio and publishing platform that has been printing for artists since 2012. They publish small-run artist books, comics, and zines, and run workshops for people who want to learn the riso themselves. Artist-friendly and deeply plugged into the local small-press scene
- **Where:** 251 Sorauren Ave, Unit 104
- **Find it:** [colourcodeprinting.com](https://colourcodeprinting.com/)

## Venues & Spaces

## Paperhouse Studio
- **What:** Experimental paper-arts studio at Artscape Youngplace, rooted in paper as a medium. Papermaking, bookbinding, and zine-friendly classes and open sessions, with a community of paper people who are generous with what they know
- **Where:** 180 Shaw St, Artscape Youngplace
- **Find it:** [paperhousestudio.com](https://paperhousestudio.com/)

## Open Studio
- **What:** Artist-run printmaking centre with a risograph membership — a RISO SF5450 EII, a technical orientation, and access included with a studio membership. A good fit for zine makers who want to print their own runs without buying a machine
- **Where:** 401 Richmond St W, Unit 104
- **Find it:** [openstudio.ca/risograph](https://openstudio.ca/risograph/)

## Art Metropole
- **What:** Artist-run centre and shop devoted to artists' books, multiples, and independent print media. Thousands of titles to browse, plus book launches and exhibitions — the place to meet Toronto's small-press and artist-book crowd
- **Where:** 896 College St
- **When:** Tuesday–Sunday 11am–6pm
- **Find it:** [artmetropole.com](https://artmetropole.com/)

## The Beguiling
- **What:** A comics institution since 1987, carrying small-press comics, zines, and artist books alongside the graphic novels. The staff picks are an education, and the shop is a regular stop for local cartoonists and zinesters
- **Where:** 319 College St
- **When:** Daily 11am–6pm
- **Find it:** [beguilingoriginalart.com](https://beguilingoriginalart.com/)
