# 🍽️ Toronto Supper Clubs & Dinner Clubs

## The Depanneur Supper Clubs
- **What:** Family-style, BYOB supper clubs cooked by a different guest chef each time, each one a different cuisine. Len Senater's ongoing culinary experiment has been running since 2011, with long communal tables and a room full of people happy to talk to the stranger next to them
- **Where:** CSI Spadina, 192 Spadina Ave (Chinatown)
- **Find it:** [thedepanneur.ca/collections/supper-clubs](https://thedepanneur.ca/collections/supper-clubs)

## Gather Supper Club
- **What:** Victoria and Steve seat a small group of strangers around one table in their family home off Queen West — cocktail hour and canapés first, then a seasonal three-course family-style dinner with wine and beer. Conversation prompts handle the awkward part
- **Where:** Queen West
- **Cost:** $120
- **Find it:** [gathersupperclubtoronto.com](https://gathersupperclubtoronto.com/)

## Rhyz Supper Club
- **What:** Michelin-trained chef Alexander Fields and wine host Julia Clarke run a monthly six-course dinner of Afro-Caribbean and Southern soul food, each menu built around one wine region with a pairing for every course. They tell the stories behind the dishes and the bottles
- **Where:** Dymon Wine Cellar, Etobicoke
- **Cost:** $180
- **Find it:** [eventbrite.ca/o/rhyz](https://www.eventbrite.ca/o/rhyz-121194204194)

## Oui Aïa
- **What:** A monthly supper club from a creative culinary studio: eight to ten tapas-style dishes and a cocktail at a private downtown address that's only revealed once you've bought a ticket. No two menus are alike
- **Where:** Downtown (address after you book)
- **Find it:** [ouiaia.com](https://www.ouiaia.com/)

## Tuba's Secret Dinner Parties
- **What:** Self-taught cook Daniel Tuba D'Souza seats about 20 strangers for six courses in a downtown apartment, with menus drawn from his travels through Korea, Sichuan, Japan and Portugal. He charges roughly what the food costs, and the waitlist runs through his TikTok
- **Where:** Downtown (address after you book)
- **Find it:** [tiktok.com/@thecookingtuba](https://www.tiktok.com/@thecookingtuba)

## Eat Out Supper Club
- **What:** A roving dinner series from queer chef and writer Sara Baron Goodman for sapphic, wlw and genderqueer people. Loud, experimental, maximalist menus at bars, farms and friends' homes — come alone or bring company, either works
- **Find it:** [instagram.com/eatoutsupperclub](https://www.instagram.com/eatoutsupperclub/)

## Saturday Spice Series
- **What:** Haem's Table and Malav Naik build each dinner around one spice, or two, so the night is part feast and part history lesson about what's on your plate. The venue is revealed to ticket holders
- **Where:** Midtown (venue after you book)
- **When:** Saturdays 7pm
- **Find it:** [tixtree.com/o/saturday-spice-series](https://www.tixtree.com/o/saturday-spice-series-f95f35c0a88b)

## Never Eat Alone
- **What:** A 7,000-member Meetup group that's been booking long restaurant tables since 2019, often two tables on the same night so you're seated with people you haven't met. You order and pay for your own dinner
- **Where:** Restaurants across Toronto
- **Find it:** [meetup.com/never-eat-alone-toronto](https://www.meetup.com/never-eat-alone-toronto/)

## Timeleft
- **What:** The app seats you with four or five strangers at a Toronto restaurant for dinner, matched on a short personality quiz. Women-only dinners run some nights
- **Find it:** [timeleft.com/toronto](https://timeleft.com/toronto/)
