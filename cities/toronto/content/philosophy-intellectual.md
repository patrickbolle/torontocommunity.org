# 🤔 Philosophy & Discussion Groups in Toronto

## The Toronto Philosophy Meetup
- **What:** A community for anyone interested in philosophy, newcomers included. Discussions, talks, reading groups, pub nights and debates across an inclusive range of topics and perspectives — online and in person
- **Find it:** [meetup.com/the-toronto-philosophy-meetup](https://www.meetup.com/the-toronto-philosophy-meetup/)

## Practical Philosophy Toronto
- **What:** Weekly philosophy discussion group built for real dialogue, not lectures. Small groups of six to eight work through one carefully chosen topic each week. No philosophy background needed, and no dogma — just thoughtful, structured conversation
- **Where:** Downtown Toronto
- **When:** Wed & Thu 7:30pm
- **Cost:** Free
- **Find it:** [practicalphilosophy.club/practical-philosophy-toronto](https://www.practicalphilosophy.club/practical-philosophy-toronto/)

## Politics, Philosophy, and Big Ideas Toronto
- **What:** A group for talking and debating philosophy, politics, art, history, technology and other big ideas. Meant for meeting like-minded people and sharing ideas freely and openly, preferably over a drink
- **Find it:** [meetup.com/poliphiloto](https://www.meetup.com/poliphiloto/)

## Toronto Debating Society
- **What:** Toronto's oldest public debating society, and a friendly forum for the lively exchange of ideas on motions from the whimsical to the serious. Parliamentary-style debates plus lighter one-on-one nights; guests welcome
- **Where:** Friends House, 60 Lowther Ave (steps from St. George Station)
- **When:** Thursdays 7:30pm
- **Find it:** [torontodebating.com](https://www.torontodebating.com/)

## Stoa of Ontario (Toronto Stoics)
- **What:** Monthly mid-month chats about Stoic philosophy and being better people. The group takes one Stoic quote or concept and works through how it applies to modern life — part seminar, part practical advice
- **When:** Monthly
- **Find it:** [meetup.com/stoa-of-ontario-in-person-toronto](https://www.meetup.com/stoa-of-ontario-in-person-toronto/)

## Skeptics in the Pub Toronto
- **What:** An informal group of scientific skeptics who get together monthly for a meal, a drink and good conversation about critical thinking, science and reason
- **Where:** North York
- **When:** Second Tuesday monthly
- **Find it:** [meetup.com/skeptics-144](https://www.meetup.com/skeptics-144/)
