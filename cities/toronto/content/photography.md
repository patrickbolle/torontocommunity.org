# 📷 Photography Clubs in Toronto

## Toronto Camera Club
- **What:** Founded in 1888, the oldest camera club in Canada and still one of the biggest in the city. Competitions, lectures, workshops, outings and meetups for every level, from first-timers to exhibition photographers
- **Find it:** [torontocameraclub.com](https://torontocameraclub.com/)

## Toronto Photo Walks
- **What:** An informal crew that photographs Toronto one walk at a time — parks, streets and neighbourhoods, with a wide mix of abilities from accomplished shooters to people just starting out. Walks are posted on the site and everyone is welcome
- **Where:** Across Toronto
- **Find it:** [torontophotowalks.ca](https://torontophotowalks.ca/)

## Toronto Digital Photography Club
- **What:** One of Canada's oldest camera clubs, founded in 1947, with a friendly mix of online and in-person meetings, image reviews, workshops and outings. Members range from beginners to working pros
- **Where:** Edithvale Community Centre, 131 Finch Avenue West
- **When:** Tuesdays 7pm
- **Find it:** [tdpc.ca](https://tdpc.ca/)

## Etobicoke Camera Club
- **What:** A west-end club for people who love photography, with Monday meetings, guest speakers, image salons and group outings. Known for being welcoming to newcomers and curious beginners
- **Where:** Etobicoke
- **When:** Mondays 7pm
- **Find it:** [etobicokecameraclub.org](https://www.etobicokecameraclub.org/)

## Mississauga Camera Club
- **What:** A big, welcoming club that has been running since 1954, with members from complete beginners to professionals. Meetings mix guest speakers, image reviews and special-interest groups
- **Where:** Lucy Turnbull Auditorium, 1389 Cawthra Road, Mississauga
- **When:** 1st & 3rd Thursday, Sept–June
- **Find it:** [mcc.mississaugacameraclub.ca](https://mcc.mississaugacameraclub.ca/)

## Chinese Canadian Photographic Society of Toronto
- **What:** A Scarborough photography society built around shared shoots, exhibitions and monthly get-togethers. Members share work through events, newsletters and online galleries
- **Where:** CCCGT, 5183 Sheppard Avenue East, Scarborough
- **When:** 2nd & last Friday 7:30pm
- **Find it:** [ccpst.com](https://ccpst.com/)

## Get Out & Shoot Toronto
- **What:** A big, active Meetup community that gets people out shooting across the city and beyond — landscapes, portraits, street photography and weekend trips. All gear and skill levels welcome, smartphones included
- **Where:** Across Toronto
- **Find it:** [meetup.com/get-out-shoot-toronto-meetup](https://www.meetup.com/get-out-shoot-toronto-meetup/)

## Beach Photo Club
- **What:** A neighbourhood club in the Beach that meets twice a month through the season for guest presentations, image sharing and group shoots. Friendly, local and open to all levels
- **Where:** Beach United Church, The Beach
- **When:** Thursday evenings, twice a month
- **Find it:** [beachphotoclub.com](https://www.beachphotoclub.com/)

## Venues & Resources

## Gallery 44 Centre for Contemporary Photography
- **What:** Artist-run centre for contemporary photography with exhibitions, talks, workshops and production facilities. A hub for Toronto's photo-art community
- **Where:** 401 Richmond Street West, Suite 120, Toronto
- **Find it:** [gallery44.org](https://www.gallery44.org/)

## CONTACT Photography Festival
- **What:** Annual citywide photography festival each May — exhibitions, public installations and talks across Toronto galleries and public spaces
- **Find it:** [contactphoto.com](https://contactphoto.com/)
