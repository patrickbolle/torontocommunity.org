# 🍄 Foraging & Nature in Toronto

## Mycological Society of Toronto
- **What:** Volunteer-run mushroom club founded in 1973, running spring and fall walks, myco-blitzes and identification sessions with guest mycologists. Beginners are welcome at every foray
- **Where:** GTA-wide
- **When:** Spring and fall foray seasons
- **Find it:** [myctor.org/forays/current](https://www.myctor.org/forays/current)

## Toronto Field Naturalists
- **What:** Volunteer naturalist club running more than 100 guided walks a year — birds, plants, trees, geology and ravines — plus a monthly public walk, lectures and a juniors program. Going since 1923
- **Where:** City-wide
- **When:** Year-round
- **Find it:** [torontofieldnaturalists.org](https://torontofieldnaturalists.org/)

## Forage & Feast: Edible and Medicinal Wild Plants of Ontario
- **What:** A friendly Meetup group of 800-plus foragers sharing wild-weed walks, foraging excursions and herbal workshops around the city. Come curious, leave with a plant list
- **Where:** Toronto
- **Find it:** [meetup.com/itsawildthingtoronto](https://www.meetup.com/itsawildthingtoronto/)

## WSC Survival School
- **What:** Ontario wilderness school whose Wild Edibles Foraging Day teaches 50-75 species of plants, trees, shrubs, ferns and lichens, with an emphasis on telling edibles from their toxic lookalikes
- **Where:** Humber Valley and Rouge Valley
- **When:** Spring to fall dates
- **Find it:** [wscsurvivalschool.com](https://wscsurvivalschool.com/event/wild-edibles-foraging-day-humber-valley-toronto-8/)

## Not Far From The Tree
- **What:** Volunteer fruit-picking charity that pairs pickers with Toronto backyards full of apples, pears, cherries and plums. A third of every harvest goes to community food programs, and picks are a sociable way to meet neighbours
- **Where:** City-wide
- **When:** Harvest season
- **Cost:** Free to volunteer
- **Find it:** [notfarfromthetree.org](https://notfarfromthetree.org/)

## High Park Nature Centre
- **What:** Non-profit nature centre in High Park running guided hikes, mushroom walks, edible and medicinal plant workshops and family programs. Small groups, knowledgeable leaders, lots of regulars
- **Where:** High Park
- **When:** Year-round
- **Find it:** [highparknaturecentre.com](https://highparknaturecentre.com/)

## Toronto Nature Stewards
- **What:** Volunteer network restoring Toronto's ravines and natural areas — pulling invasive plants, planting natives and learning plant ID from trained lead stewards. Hands-on conservation with a genuinely social crew
- **Where:** 50-plus sites across Toronto
- **Cost:** Free to volunteer
- **Find it:** [torontonaturestewards.org](https://torontonaturestewards.org/)

## Lost Rivers
- **What:** Volunteer-led walks that trace Toronto's buried creeks and ravine systems, mixing natural history with the story of the city built over them. A good way to learn the watersheds under your feet
- **Where:** Don Valley and city-wide
- **When:** Seasonal walk schedule
- **Find it:** [lostrivers.ca](https://lostrivers.ca/)

## Venues & Resources

### Rouge National Urban Park
- **What:** Canada's first national urban park, with trails, wetlands and farmland across Scarborough and Markham. Parks Canada runs free guided walks and tree and plant identification hikes
- **Where:** Scarborough
- **Find it:** [parks.canada.ca/pn-np/on/rouge](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/on/rouge)

### Tommy Thompson Park
- **What:** A 250-hectare accidental wilderness on the Leslie Street Spit, with trails, shoreline and a bird research station. One of the best places in the city to watch nature reclaim a landscape
- **Where:** Leslie Street Spit
- **Find it:** [tommythompsonpark.ca](https://tommythompsonpark.ca/)

### Evergreen Brick Works
- **What:** A restored Don Valley brick factory turned community nature hub, with trails, gardens, a farmers' market and outdoor programming for all ages
- **Where:** Don Valley
- **Find it:** [evergreen.ca](https://www.evergreen.ca/)

### Toronto and Region Conservation Authority
- **What:** Manages conservation areas, nature centres and volunteer planting and restoration days across the GTA. The place to look for a trail, a stewardship day or a guided walk outside the city core
- **Find it:** [trca.ca](https://trca.ca/)

### Toronto Urban Growers
- **What:** Free network of Toronto urban farmers, gardeners and composters, with a busy Google group for events, questions and swaps. A good first stop if you want to grow or forage with other people
- **Find it:** [torontourbangrowers.org](https://torontourbangrowers.org/)
