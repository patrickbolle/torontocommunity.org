# 🌿 Volunteer in Toronto

## Daily Bread Food Bank
- **What:** One of Toronto's biggest food banks, moving donated food out to member agencies from a warehouse in South Etobicoke. Volunteers sort and repack food, pick agency orders, prep soups and casseroles in the kitchen, or help at the welcome centre, weekday shifts with a roll-up-your-sleeves, sociable crew
- **Where:** 191 New Toronto Street, South Etobicoke
- **When:** Weekday shifts
- **Find it:** [dailybread.ca/volunteer](https://www.dailybread.ca/volunteer/)

## Second Harvest
- **What:** Canada's largest food rescue organization, redirecting surplus food from grocers and restaurants to non-profits across the GTA. Individual volunteers take year-round warehouse, driver-assistant and food-sort roles, including a three-hour Food Sort Team Leader shift on Tuesdays to Thursdays
- **Where:** Toronto
- **When:** Year-round
- **Find it:** [secondharvest.ca/volunteer](https://www.secondharvest.ca/volunteer/)

## The Stop Community Food Centre
- **What:** A busy community food centre running a food bank, drop-in meals, community kitchens, a farmers' market and urban agriculture programs. Volunteers help keep all of it going, a warm, hands-on place where neighbours cook and eat together rather than just pick up a hamper
- **Where:** 1884 Davenport Road
- **Find it:** [thestop.org/volunteer](https://www.thestop.org/volunteer/)

## Habitat for Humanity GTA
- **What:** Builds affordable homes across the Greater Toronto Area and runs the ReStores that fund the work. Volunteers aged 16-plus can join a build-site crew, help customers and sort donations in a ReStore, or come out with a corporate team
- **Where:** GTA-wide
- **Find it:** [habitatgta.ca/volunteer](https://habitatgta.ca/volunteer/)

## Fred Victor
- **What:** A Toronto charity working on housing, shelter, health and food security, with programs concentrated in the city's east end. Individual volunteers attend an orientation, commit to at least a month and mostly help out on weekdays between 8am and 3pm, grounded, direct work alongside people facing homelessness
- **Where:** East Toronto
- **When:** Weekdays 8am, 3pm
- **Find it:** [fredvictor.org/individual-volunteer-opportunities](https://www.fredvictor.org/individual-volunteer-opportunities/)

## Scott Mission
- **What:** A Christian ministry of mercy that has served meals, shelter, groceries and clothing in downtown Toronto since 1941. Volunteers work in the commercial kitchen, the food and clothing bank, the reheat kitchen or evening shelter meals, a structured program with training on your first shift
- **Where:** 502 Spadina Avenue
- **When:** Weekday shifts
- **Find it:** [scottmission.com/volunteer-opportunities](https://scottmission.com/get-involved/volunteer/volunteer-opportunities/)

## Toronto Humane Society
- **What:** One of the city's largest animal welfare organizations, with more than 400 volunteers caring for cats, dogs and special species, running tours and supporting the Pet Parent Support Network. Roles ask for a weekly shift over at least six months, with hands-on training provided
- **Where:** 11 River Street
- **Find it:** [torontohumanesociety.com/become-a-volunteer](https://www.torontohumanesociety.com/support-us/become-a-volunteer/)
- **Notes:** Applications are paused during high-volume periods, check the page for the next intake

## Toronto and Region Conservation Authority
- **What:** The conservation authority for Toronto's watersheds, running planting days, invasive-species pulls and stewardship events at parks and conservation areas across the GTA. Volunteers can also join the Conservation Youth Corps for high-school community hours or the Girls Can Too program
- **Where:** GTA-wide
- **Find it:** [trca.ca/get-involved/volunteer](https://trca.ca/get-involved/volunteer/)

## The 519
- **What:** The 2SLGBTQ+ community centre at Church and Wellesley, with programs, events and advocacy running all year. Volunteers aged 18-plus attend a mandatory orientation, then take recurring weekly roles in programs or sign up for one-off event shifts, no minimum monthly hours
- **Where:** Church & Wellesley
- **When:** Weekly roles
- **Find it:** [the519.org/volunteer](https://www.the519.org/volunteer)

## Ve'ahavta
- **What:** A Jewish social-action organization helping people facing poverty and homelessness. Volunteers ride the Mobile Jewish Response to Homelessness outreach van on weekday evenings, prep meals in the commercial kitchen or sort donations in the warehouse, with clear shift lengths and one-off options too
- **Where:** North York & downtown
- **When:** Weekday evening van shifts
- **Find it:** [veahavta.org/volunteer](https://veahavta.org/volunteer/)

## Venues & Resources

### Evergreen Brick Works
- **What:** A restored Don Valley brick factory turned community hub, with gardens, trails and a year-round market. Its volunteer program runs gardening and stewardship sessions day and evening through the week, plus public-market shifts and children's programs
- **Where:** 550 Bayview Avenue
- **Find it:** [evergreen.ca/take-action/volunteer](https://www.evergreen.ca/take-action/volunteer/)

### City of Toronto. Volunteer with the City
- **What:** The City's own volunteer listings for programs, events and services, park stewardship, community recreation, animal fostering, cultural events and seasonal initiatives. A good starting point for a short-term or seasonal role with the city
- **Find it:** [toronto.ca/volunteer-with-the-city](https://www.toronto.ca/community-people/get-involved/volunteer-with-the-city/)

### Toronto Nature Stewards
- **What:** A volunteer network restoring ravines and natural areas at 53 public sites across the city, removing harmful plants, planting natives, picking up trash and teaching neighbours about the ecosystems on their doorstep
- **Where:** 53 sites across Toronto
- **Find it:** [torontonaturestewards.org](https://torontonaturestewards.org/)

### Not Far From The Tree
- **What:** A fruit-picking charity that has matched volunteers with Toronto backyards since 2008, harvesting apples, pears, cherries and plums. A third of every pick goes to community groups tackling food insecurity, and the picks are a sociable way to meet neighbours
- **Where:** City-wide
- **When:** Harvest season
- **Find it:** [notfarfromthetree.org](https://notfarfromthetree.org/)

### High Park Nature Centre
- **What:** A non-profit nature centre in High Park whose volunteers help run nature programs for kids, families and adults, and learn the black oak savannah along the way. Volunteers must be 15-plus and attend an orientation
- **Where:** High Park
- **Find it:** [highparknaturecentre.com/volunteer](https://highparknaturecentre.com/volunteer/)
