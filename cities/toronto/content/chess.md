# ♟️ Chess Clubs in Toronto

## Annex Chess Club
- **What:** Casual games and a weekly Swiss tournament under the motto "Chess For Everyone!" Founded in 2010, it hosts the Ontario Open and Toronto Open and is home to the Toronto Women's Chess Club
- **Where:** 918 Bathurst Street, The Annex
- **When:** Mondays 7–11pm
- **Find it:** [annexchessclub.com](https://annexchessclub.com/)

## Hart House Chess Club
- **What:** The University of Toronto's official club, running since 1895 and the oldest chess club in Canada. Casual play, novice lessons and open tournaments in a friendly, lively room — students and community members welcome
- **Where:** Hart House Reading Room, 7 Hart House Circle
- **When:** Fridays 4–11pm
- **Cost:** $10/semester for U of T students
- **Find it:** [harthousechess.com](https://harthousechess.com/)

## Chess in the Park
- **What:** Free weekend chess outdoors in Toronto's parks through the summer, moving indoors when it gets cold. A relaxed, all-comers crowd that explores the city's chess tables
- **Where:** Parks across Toronto — location changes weekly
- **When:** Weekends
- **Cost:** Free
- **Find it:** [torontochess.ca/clubs/chess-in-the-park](https://www.torontochess.ca/clubs/chess-in-the-park)

## Beaches Chess Club
- **What:** Free, volunteer-run club with casual games, blitz tournaments and instructional workshops. Indoors at Beaches Sandbox in winter, at the Leuty Boathouse in Kew Gardens in summer
- **Where:** Beaches Sandbox, 2181 Queen Street East
- **When:** Sundays 2–5pm
- **Cost:** Free
- **Find it:** [torontochess.ca/clubs/beaches-chess-club](https://www.torontochess.ca/clubs/beaches-chess-club)

## Scarborough Chess Club
- **What:** Volunteer-run and founded in 1960, one of Canada's longest-running community chess clubs. FIDE- and CFC-rated Swiss tournaments with sections for every strength; boards, pieces and clocks provided
- **Where:** Birkdale Community Centre, 1299 Ellesmere Road
- **When:** Thursdays 6:30–11pm, Sept–June
- **Find it:** [scarboroughchessclub.ca](https://scarboroughchessclub.ca/)

## Willowdale Chess Club
- **What:** Friendly volunteer-run North York club going 40-plus years, with Tuesday regular play, a Saturday drop-in and a kids' club
- **Where:** Earl Bales Community Centre, 4169 Bathurst Street
- **When:** Tuesdays 6–9pm
- **Cost:** $10/year
- **Find it:** [willowdalechess.blogspot.com](https://willowdalechess.blogspot.com/)

## Etobicoke Chess Club
- **What:** Café chess nights with a "Play for fun, Play for real, Play for free" ethos. Casual games most Fridays, plus free CFC-rated rapid tournaments through the year
- **Where:** Second Cup, 265 Wincott Drive, Etobicoke
- **When:** Fridays 6–9pm
- **Cost:** Free
- **Find it:** [etobicokechessclub.wordpress.com](https://etobicokechessclub.wordpress.com/)

## Mississauga Chess Club
- **What:** Canada's biggest chess club, with players from beginner to master. Adult tournament nights plus casual games, and a large youth program across the week
- **Where:** Erindale United Church, 1444 Dundas Crescent, Mississauga
- **When:** Wednesdays 6:30–10:30pm (adult club)
- **Find it:** [mississaugachessclub.ca](https://www.mississaugachessclub.ca/)

## Venues & Resources

## Greater Toronto Chess League
- **What:** The league connecting GTA clubs — a directory of every member club with locations and schedules, plus tournament listings
- **Find it:** [torontochess.ca/clubs](https://www.torontochess.ca/clubs)

## Toronto Chess Centre
- **What:** Non-profit chess hub running tournaments, qualifiers and lessons from downtown and north Toronto locations, with a busy events calendar
- **Where:** Downtown Toronto and Thornhill
- **Find it:** [torontochesscentre.com](https://torontochesscentre.com/)

## Chess Institute of Canada
- **What:** Toronto charity teaching chess through school clubs, camps and classes for kids and seniors — a good landing spot if you want to learn rather than compete
- **Where:** 918 Bathurst Street
- **Find it:** [chessinstitute.ca](https://chessinstitute.ca/)
