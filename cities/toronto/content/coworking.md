## Venues & Spaces

## Centre for Social Innovation (CSI)
- **What:** Canada's largest social innovation community, with hot desks, dedicated desks and private offices at its Spadina hub. Nonprofits, artists and impact founders fill the room, and the events calendar is the real draw, come for the desk, stay for the collaborators
- **Where:** 192 Spadina Ave (Spadina & Richmond)
- **Find it:** [socialinnovation.org/coworking](https://socialinnovation.org/coworking/)

## Acme Works
- **What:** A warm, dog-friendly coworking house for freelancers, remote workers and small teams, with shared harvest tables, private offices and meeting rooms. Regulars mention making actual friends here, and the shared tables make it easy to strike up a conversation
- **Where:** 229 Niagara St (West End)
- **Find it:** [acmeworks.ca](https://acmeworks.ca)

## Workhaus
- **What:** Canadian-owned coworking with a cluster of downtown Toronto locations, from hot desks to private offices. Shared spaces and a community-driven ethos aim to make a big-city workday feel less anonymous
- **Where:** 901 King St W, plus 215 Spadina Ave and other downtown locations
- **Find it:** [workhaus.ca](https://workhaus.ca)

## Workplace One
- **What:** Flexible coworking and private offices across several Toronto work centres, pitched at professionals who want a quieter, well-run space. A friendly crowd of small teams and independents, with hot desks alongside private offices and meeting rooms
- **Where:** 340 King St E (King East)
- **Find it:** [workplaceone.ca](https://www.workplaceone.ca)

## Adelaide House Coworking
- **What:** Boutique coworking in a converted garment-cutting warehouse in the Fashion District, tall ceilings, warm wood and a lounge that feels like home. Hot desks, dedicated desks and day passes, with a smaller, homey feel than the big coworking chains
- **Where:** 410 Adelaide St W (Fashion District)
- **Cost:** $40 day pass
- **Find it:** [adelaidehousecoworking.com](https://adelaidehousecoworking.com)

## Miraki Life Workspace
- **What:** A creative studio and coworking space in Roncesvalles with 14-foot ceilings and street access. Day and night passes, workshops and events draw a welcoming mix of creatives, producers and remote teams
- **Where:** 35 Golden Ave, Unit 114 (Roncesvalles)
- **Find it:** [mirakilifeworkspace.com](https://mirakilifeworkspace.com)

## ACE Coworking
- **What:** A community-minded coworking space in Oakville for entrepreneurs, startups, freelancers and remote workers. Hot desks, dedicated desks and private offices, with member events and networking built into the membership
- **Where:** 132 Trafalgar Rd, Oakville
- **Find it:** [acecoworking.ca](https://acecoworking.ca)

## Touchdown Coworking
- **What:** A friendly coworking hub serving Oakville and Mississauga, with hot desks, dedicated desks, private offices and day passes. Its monthly Crown & Community networking breakfast is a low-pressure way to meet other local independents
- **Where:** 2030 Bristol Circle, Suite 210, Oakville
- **Find it:** [touchdowncoworking.com](https://touchdowncoworking.com)
