# 🪷 Mindfulness & Meditation in Toronto

## Toronto Zen Centre
- **What:** Zen Buddhist community founded in 1967 and the oldest Zen group in Canada. Daily zazen sittings, introductory workshops and courses in a quiet High Park house, newcomers start with the Introduction to Zen workshop
- **Where:** 33 High Park Gardens, High Park
- **When:** Daily sittings
- **Find it:** [torontozen.org](https://torontozen.org)

## Kadampa Meditation Centre Canada
- **What:** Kadampa Buddhist temple and meditation centre in Little Italy, with drop-in classes, guided meditations, talks and retreats. A large, welcoming community for beginners and experienced meditators alike
- **Where:** 631 Crawford St, Little Italy
- **When:** Weekly drop-in classes
- **Find it:** [meditateintoronto.org](https://www.meditateintoronto.org)

## Mindfulness Practice Community of Toronto
- **What:** Interfaith sangha in the Plum Village tradition of Thich Nhat Hanh, practicing together since 1997. Weekly sittings, in-person sangha and special events, with newcomers free to drop in
- **Where:** Roncesvalles United Church, 214 Wright Ave
- **When:** Saturdays 9:30-11:30am
- **Find it:** [mindfulnesspracticecommunity.org](https://mindfulnesspracticecommunity.org)

## DDM Toronto Centre
- **What:** Chan Buddhist community in the Dharma Drum lineage, practicing in Scarborough since 1998. Regular meditation, chanting, study groups and dharma teachings, including a Wednesday night sit with Eight Form Moving Meditation
- **Where:** 1025 McNicoll Ave, Scarborough
- **When:** Wednesdays 7pm
- **Find it:** [ddmbaontario.org](https://www.ddmbaontario.org)

## Nalandabodhi Toronto
- **What:** Tibetan Buddhist community in the Nyingma tradition with a centre in Leslieville. Meditation practice, Buddhist philosophy classes and visiting-teacher events, offered in person and over Zoom
- **Where:** 1159 Dundas St E, #153, Leslieville
- **When:** Saturdays 10am, online
- **Find it:** [toronto.nalandabodhi.ca](https://toronto.nalandabodhi.ca)

## Theravada Buddhist Community
- **What:** Toronto-based lay Theravada sangha that meets every Sunday for chanting, a 45-minute silent sit and a reading or recorded talk from a Theravada teacher, with discussion and an optional lunch nearby. No experience needed, just the willingness to sit quietly
- **Where:** Carrot Common, 348 Danforth Ave, Room 212
- **When:** Sundays 10am, 12pm
- **Find it:** [theravadabuddhistcommunity.org](https://www.theravadabuddhistcommunity.org)

## Snow Lion Meditation Community
- **What:** The merged Toronto Mindfulness Community and Endless Ground, now one community. Weekly sittings in Toronto and online, plus MBSR and advanced mindfulness courses, workshops and retreats
- **Where:** Toronto and online
- **When:** Weekly classes
- **Find it:** [snowlionmeditationcommunity.org](https://snowlionmeditationcommunity.org/)

## Tergar Toronto
- **What:** Practice group following Mingyur Rinpoche's Joy of Living program, with guided and unguided meditation and group discussion. Hybrid sessions draw a warm, unpretentious crowd
- **Where:** The Centering Space, 59 Cambridge Ave
- **When:** 2nd & 4th Monday, 7-8:30pm
- **Find it:** [tergar.org/toronto](https://tergar.org/toronto)

## Brahma Kumaris Toronto
- **What:** Raja Yoga meditation centre at Inspiration House, offering courses, talks, workshops and group meditations. A diverse, all-ages community where every program is offered free
- **Where:** 3000 Islington Ave
- **When:** World Meditation Hour, third Sunday monthly
- **Cost:** Free
- **Find it:** [brahmakumaris.ca/toronto](https://www.brahmakumaris.ca/toronto)

## Heartfulness Toronto
- **What:** Heartfulness meditation centre for Toronto and the GTA, part of the Sahaj Marg tradition. Weekly group sittings, individual sittings and regional gatherings in a dedicated Mississauga space
- **Where:** 1337 Matheson Blvd E, Mississauga
- **When:** Wednesdays 7:30pm and Sundays 9am
- **Find it:** [heartfulness.org Toronto centre](https://preceptor.heartfulness.org/en/smww/ashrams/toronto/general-info)

## Yoga Meditation Canada
- **What:** Free meditation classes and community gatherings across the GTA, from downtown Toronto to Etobicoke, Scarborough and Markham. Sessions use breathing, mantra and group kirtan, and are designed for beginners; the wider calendar adds kirtans and vegetarian feasts
- **Where:** Greater Toronto Area
- **When:** Varies by location
- **Cost:** Free
- **Find it:** [yogameditation.ca](https://yogameditation.ca/free-meditation-classes-greater-toronto-area/)

## Consciousness Explorers Club
- **What:** Meditation community committed to pluralism in practice, welcoming everyone from absolute beginners to longtime meditators. Weekly Monday night sits plus courses, retreats and social events, with a playful, experimental spirit
- **Where:** St Stephen-in-the-Fields, 365 College St, near Kensington Market
- **When:** Mondays 7:30pm
- **Find it:** [meetup.com/consciousness-explorers-club](https://www.meetup.com/consciousness-explorers-club/)

## Venues & Resources

Several of these groups meet in borrowed community spaces rather than dedicated studios. Roncesvalles United Church (214 Wright Ave), the Carrot Common (348 Danforth Ave), The Centering Space (59 Cambridge Ave) and St Stephen-in-the-Fields (365 College St) all host regular sits. The Buddhist centres above also welcome visitors to their own meditation halls and shrines; check each calendar before you go.
