# 🤹 Circus, Juggling & Flow Arts in Toronto

## Toronto Circus Centre
- **What:** Circus school running adult aerial hoop, silks and beginner/intermediate aerial classes, plus circus fitness and flexibility, alongside kids' and teen programs. "Prepare to be amazed by what you can do"
- **Where:** Leaside United Church, 822 Millwood Rd, Leaside
- **Find it:** [torontocircuscentre.com](https://www.torontocircuscentre.com/)

## The Circus Fix
- **What:** Aerial and ground circus studio with "an inclusive, friendly environment where everyone is encouraged to explore, play, and challenge themselves" — and a supportive community ready to cheer you on. Classes in silks, hoop, trapeze, hammock, straps, acro, handstands, flow acrobatics and flexibility, plus open gym
- **Where:** 17 Carlaw Ave, East End, and 950 Dupont St, West End
- **Find it:** [thecircusfix.ca](https://thecircusfix.ca/)

## Balance Studio
- **What:** Small-group aerial studio built around aerial hoop, aerial dance and mat-based conditioning, with clear progressions and a calm, welcoming room to move. Beginner adult aerial hoop classes are the entry point
- **Where:** North York
- **Find it:** [balancestudio.ca](https://www.balancestudio.ca/)

## Aura Aerial Art Centre
- **What:** Aerial hammock, spinning hammock, aerial hoop and mat yoga studio. "Move with Confidence. Flow with Intention." New-student offer, workshops and aerial teacher training
- **Where:** 30 Drewry Ave, North York
- **Find it:** [auraaerialartcentre.com](https://www.auraaerialartcentre.com/)

## Acro Labs
- **What:** AcroYoga and partner-acrobatics classes and workshops — partner stretching, inversions and creative movement — led by certified AcroYoga teacher Abbey Nydam. "Move Together, Grow Together"
- **Where:** 790 Queen St W, West Queen West
- **Find it:** [acrodanceandmove.com](https://www.acrodanceandmove.com/)

## Cirque-It Work-It
- **What:** GTA circus school for ages 5 to adult, with beginner-friendly classes and a "circus is for everyone" ethos. Builds "confidence, strength, friendships" at your own pace
- **Where:** 3-500 Hensall Cir, Mississauga
- **Cost:** Free trial class
- **Find it:** [cirqueitworkit.ca](https://www.cirqueitworkit.ca/)
