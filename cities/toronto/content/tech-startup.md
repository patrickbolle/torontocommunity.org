# 💼 Tech & Startup Meetups in Toronto

## TechTO
- **What:** Toronto's flagship tech community, a monthly town hall ("Together Toronto") that packs the room with founders, operators, builders, students, and investors, plus smaller dinners and socials.
- **When:** Monthly
- **Find it:** [techto.org](https://www.techto.org/)

## Startup Grind Toronto
- **What:** Toronto chapter of the global founder network, fireside chats, founder/investor mixers, and a founder-first community for early-stage builders.
- **Find it:** [startupgrind.com/toronto](https://www.startupgrind.com/toronto/)

## FoundersBeta
- **What:** Founder community for connecting with cofounders, talent, and funding. Hosts "Founders Collab" meetups and keeps a running list of Toronto startup events.
- **Find it:** [foundersbeta.com](https://www.foundersbeta.com/)

## Founder Social
- **What:** A casual dinner group for people building or running something in Toronto, tech startups, small businesses, online stores. "Founder" is defined broadly, and everyone's welcome.
- **Find it:** [meetup.com/founder-social-startup-builders-business-owners](https://www.meetup.com/founder-social-startup-builders-business-owners/)

## AI Tinkerers Toronto
- **What:** Meetups for people actively building with AI, hands-on demos, talks, and show-and-tell by technical founders and engineers.
- **Find it:** [toronto.aitinkerers.org](https://toronto.aitinkerers.org/)

## GenAI Collective Toronto
- **What:** AI founder and investor community, fireside chats and mixers for the generative AI ecosystem, from students to founders to VCs.
- **Find it:** [genaicollective.ai](https://www.genaicollective.ai/)

## Toronto Machine Learning Society (TMLS)
- **What:** ML and AI community running meetups, micro-summits, and the annual TMLS conference. Industry and research, open to all levels.
- **Find it:** [torontomachinelearning.com](https://www.torontomachinelearning.com/)

## TorontoJS
- **What:** Volunteer-run JavaScript community (since 2010), talks and tech-talks with drinks and snacks, plus a supportive crew for everyone building in JS.
- **Find it:** [torontojs.com](https://torontojs.com/)

## PyLadies Toronto
- **What:** Python mentorship group for women and non-binary folks, evening talks and networking, welcoming to people who've never programmed through to pros.
- **Find it:** [meetup.com/PyLadies-Toronto](https://www.meetup.com/PyLadies-Toronto/)

## GDG Toronto
- **What:** Google Developer Group. DevFest Toronto plus talks and hands-on labs on AI, machine learning, and cloud, with hundreds of local builders.
- **Find it:** [gdg.community.dev/gdg-toronto](https://gdg.community.dev/gdg-toronto/)

## Toronto Ruby
- **What:** Ruby and Rails meetup, evening talks hosted at local tech companies, plus socials for the Ruby community.
- **Find it:** [meetup.com/toronto-ruby](https://www.meetup.com/toronto-ruby/)

## Go Toronto
- **What:** Meetup for Go (Golang) developers, talks and networking for people building with Go.
- **Find it:** [meetup.com/go-toronto](https://www.meetup.com/go-toronto/)

## Toronto Product Managers
- **What:** Community for product managers and PM-curious folks, talks, panels, and networking across Toronto's product scene.
- **Find it:** [meetup.com/toronto-product-managers](https://www.meetup.com/toronto-product-managers/)

## Toronto Rust
- **What:** Rust meetup for programmers working with or curious about Rust, a talk, then help getting unstuck on your own project.
- **Find it:** [meetup.com/TorontoRust](https://www.meetup.com/TorontoRust/)

## DMZ (Toronto Metropolitan University)
- **What:** University tech incubator with a packed events calendar, founder meetups, workshops, and pitch nights open to the wider ecosystem.
- **Find it:** [dmz.torontomu.ca](https://dmz.torontomu.ca/)

## Venues & Resources

## BrainStation Toronto
- **What:** Tech bootcamp with a downtown campus, runs workshops and industry events where you'll meet other learners and practitioners.
- **Find it:** [brainstation.io/toronto](https://brainstation.io/toronto)

## Canada Learning Code
- **What:** National org with a Toronto chapter running in-person, beginner-friendly coding workshops and meetups for adults and youth.
- **Find it:** [canadalearningcode.ca](https://www.canadalearningcode.ca/)
