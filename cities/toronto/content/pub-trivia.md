# 🧠 Pub Trivia Nights in Toronto

*Mostly weekly nights and the hosts who run them. Know a trivia night we should add? [Submit it here.](/submit/)*

## Quizmaster Jess
- **What:** Jess and her team run general and themed trivia across the city, from free general-knowledge quizzes you book ahead to ticketed themed nights. Regulars follow the host from room to room, and every venue gets a proper quiz night rather than a side attraction.
- **Where:** Saint John's Tavern, Local Public Eatery Leaside, The Drake Hotel, Cassette at Gladstone House, Black Sheep, Eastbound Brewing, Brunswick Bierworks and The Rec Room
- **When:** Weekly, most nights
- **Cost:** Free general trivia
- **Find it:** [quizmasterjess.com/trivia-locations](https://www.quizmasterjess.com/trivia-locations)

## Are You Smarter Than a Scottish Guy
- **What:** Simon has been writing and hosting Toronto trivia since 2015, with a brand-new game every week and some rounds hiding a connection between the answers. Three weekly pub nights, including what the host calls Canada's longest-running queer trivia night.
- **Where:** Brazen Head (Liberty Village), One Eyed Jack (Entertainment District), Pegasus on Church (Church-Wellesley)
- **When:** Mondays, Tuesdays & Thursdays 7pm
- **Find it:** [areyousmarterthanascottishguy.com](https://www.areyousmarterthanascottishguy.com/)

## The Sociable Host
- **What:** A Toronto trivia company that builds weekly pub quizzes and private events for bars around the city, with a friendly, fast-moving format and a broad mix of movies, music, history and pop culture. Their public nights include the bi-weekly Steam Whistle Roundhouse game.
- **Where:** Steam Whistle Kitchen and venues across Toronto
- **When:** Every other Tuesday 7:30pm
- **Find it:** [thesociablehost.com](https://thesociablehost.com/)

## ruby PUB TRIVIA
- **What:** A GTA pub-quiz outfit with a fully visual, audio-driven show, phone scoring and a hostmaster running the room. They play at bars and breweries across Etobicoke, Mississauga, Oakville, Brampton and Toronto, so there's usually a game a short drive from home.
- **Where:** Etobicoke, Mississauga, Oakville, Brampton and across the GTA
- **When:** Weekly, Monday, Thursday & Sunday
- **Find it:** [rubypubtrivia.ca](https://www.rubypubtrivia.ca/)

## The Rhino
- **What:** A Parkdale bar and grill that turns Monday nights into a proper weekly quiz. Grab a table, order a pint and settle in, the pub posts the latest news and announcements on Instagram.
- **Where:** 1249 Queen St W, Parkdale
- **When:** Mondays 8pm
- **Find it:** [therhino.ca/trivia](https://www.therhino.ca/trivia)

## C'est What
- **What:** A long-running Old Town brewpub downstairs at Front and Church, with a Wednesday trivia night in its own events calendar. Craft beer, pub fare and a central location that works for an after-work team.
- **Where:** 67 Front St E, Old Town
- **When:** Wednesdays
- **Find it:** [cestwhat.com/trivia](https://www.cestwhat.com/trivia)

## Steam Whistle Roundhouse
- **What:** Bi-weekly trivia in the Steam Whistle Kitchen inside the historic John Street Roundhouse, with the CN Tower out the window. Teams of any size, questions spanning movies, music, history and sports, and a Pint & Pretzel combo before kickoff.
- **Where:** 255 Bremner Blvd, downtown
- **When:** Every other Tuesday 7:30pm
- **Find it:** [steamwhistle.ca. Tuesday trivia](https://steamwhistle.ca/blogs/news/trivia-night-toronto-tuesday)

## Hemingway's
- **What:** A Yorkville pub that has called the neighbourhood home for 42 years and leans into themed trivia. The Office, Name That Tune, Harry Potter, Star Wars and 90s nights. Rooftop patio, New Zealand pub food and a crowd that comes ready to play.
- **Where:** 142 Cumberland St, Yorkville
- **When:** Weekly themed trivia
- **Find it:** [hemingways.to/events](https://hemingways.to/events)

## Snakes & Lattes Annex
- **What:** The board game café's Annex location runs general trivia every Tuesday, with a $50 gift card for the winning team and $5 pints and cans. A low-stakes room for mixed-interest groups.
- **Where:** 600 Bloor St W, the Annex
- **When:** Tuesdays 7-9pm
- **Find it:** [snakesandlattes.com/events-toronto](https://www.snakesandlattes.com/events-toronto)

## The Stone Lion
- **What:** A Beaches pub with a Tuesday quiz from Quiz Coconut, the British-style quiz outfit that has run a weekly game here since 2016. Heated patio, craft beer and a neighbourhood crowd that takes the game just seriously enough.
- **Where:** 1958 Queen St E, the Beaches
- **When:** Tuesdays 7:30pm
- **Find it:** [stonelionpub.com/trivia](https://stonelionpub.com/trivia)

## Venues & Resources

## Bellwoods Brewery
- **What:** The Ossington brewery's Monday trivia is first come, first served, with prizes and a friendly room. Get there early, order something from the kitchen and settle in for a long one.
- **Where:** 124 Ossington Ave, Trinity-Bellwoods
- **When:** Mondays 7pm
- **Find it:** [bellwoodsbrewery.com](https://www.bellwoodsbrewery.com/)

## Nickel Brook Brewing Co.
- **What:** ruby PUB TRIVIA's Wednesday night in Etobicoke, hosted by Bill Cooke, with a big tap list and a proper local crowd. Reservations on Eventbrite don't hold a table, so call the brewery to lock one in.
- **Where:** 1589 The Queensway, Etobicoke
- **When:** Wednesdays 7:30pm
- **Cost:** Free
- **Find it:** [eventbrite.ca. Wednesday Night Trivia at Nickel Brook](https://www.eventbrite.ca/e/wednesday-night-trivia-at-nickel-brook-brewing-co-in-etobicoke-tickets-1999855848678)
