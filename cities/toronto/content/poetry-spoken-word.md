# 🎤 Poetry & Spoken Word in Toronto

## Toronto Poetry Slam
- **What:** Toronto's flagship poetry slam, a twice-monthly competition and open mic run by the Toronto Poetry Project since 2005. One of Canada's largest spoken word events, with 60% of performance slots reserved for Black and Indigenous poets
- **Where:** The Drake Underground, 1150 Queen St W
- **When:** Twice monthly
- **Find it:** [torontopoetryslam.ca](https://www.torontopoetryslam.ca/)

## Shab-e She'r (Poetry Night)
- **What:** Canada's most diverse poetry reading and open mic, with featured poets and an open mic in any language. Multilingual, brave and warmly hosted
- **Where:** Tranzac Club, 292 Brunswick Ave
- **When:** Monthly
- **Find it:** [facebook.com/ShabeSherTO](https://www.facebook.com/ShabeSherTO/)

## Art Bar Poetry Series
- **What:** Canada's longest-running poetry-only weekly reading series, running since 1991, three featured poets plus an open mic. A cozy, literary room that's welcoming to newcomers
- **Where:** Free Times Cafe, 320 College St
- **When:** Tuesdays 8pm
- **Find it:** [facebook.com/ArtBarPoetrySeries](https://www.facebook.com/ArtBarPoetrySeries/)

## Spoken Soul Collective
- **What:** A collective of award-winning spoken word artists and curators. Paulina O'Kieffe-Anthony, Dwayne Morgan and Randell Adjei, producing the SpokenSoul Festival, The Gathering and 12 Hrs of Black Literature
- **Where:** Scarborough
- **Find it:** [spokensoulcollective.com](https://www.spokensoulcollective.com/)

## Sauga Poetry
- **What:** Mississauga's hub for spoken word, a monthly open mic plus occasional slams and writing workshops. A supportive, community-first room for emerging poets
- **Where:** Mississauga
- **When:** Monthly
- **Find it:** [Sauga Poetry on Eventbrite](https://www.eventbrite.com/o/61588622493)

## Embrace Queer Open Mic
- **What:** Toronto's longest-running queer and trans all-ages open mic, welcoming poetry, spoken word, comedy, music, drag and storytelling. Warm, mixed and endlessly encouraging
- **Where:** Glad Day Lit, 32 Lisgar St
- **When:** Monthly
- **Find it:** [@embraceopenmic](https://www.instagram.com/embraceopenmic/)

## Poetry Brothel Toronto
- **What:** An immersive literary cabaret where poets perform as characters and give private readings, plus a monthly Poetry Club with timed writing exercises and sharing. Theatrical, playful and open to all levels
- **Where:** The Wych, 1150 Davenport Rd
- **When:** First Tuesday monthly
- **Find it:** [brendeegreen.com](https://www.brendeegreen.com/whatsonpoetrybrotheltoronto)

## Nukkad Toronto
- **What:** A South Asian open mic series for poetry, spoken word, storytelling and music, with four-minute sets and a warm, big-hearted crowd
- **Where:** Harmony Cafe, 75 Nassau St
- **Find it:** [@nukkadtoronto](https://www.instagram.com/nukkadtoronto/)

## Brampton Arts Organization
- **What:** Brampton's arts organization, behind the Brampton Poetry Project, poems by local writers on buses and transit stops across the city, plus open mics, workshops and artist development for the city's poets
- **Where:** Brampton
- **Find it:** [bramptonartsorg.ca](https://bramptonartsorg.ca/)

## Venues & Spaces

## Understory
- **What:** A trans-owned cafe and wine bar in the Church-Wellesley Village, hosting poetry nights, trans open mics, book swaps and other queer community events. Cozy and low-key
- **Where:** 505 Church St
- **Find it:** [understorycafe.com](https://understorycafe.com/)

## Glad Day Bookshop
- **What:** Toronto's iconic queer bookstore and event space, home to open mics like Embrace, author readings and book launches. A Village institution
- **Where:** 32 Lisgar St
- **Find it:** [gladday.ca](https://www.gladday.ca/)

## Free Times Cafe
- **What:** A College Street cafe and live-music room, and the longtime home of the Art Bar Poetry Series. Comfort food, a small stage and a literary crowd
- **Where:** 320 College St
- **Find it:** [freetimescafe.com](https://www.freetimescafe.com/)
