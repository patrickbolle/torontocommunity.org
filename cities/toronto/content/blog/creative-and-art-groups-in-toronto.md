There's something disarming about making art next to strangers. Nobody's performing, everyone's a little vulnerable, and by the end you've made a friend and a bad drawing you're weirdly proud of. Toronto has a deep bench of these rooms — and almost none of them care whether you're any good.

Here's where to draw, sketch, print and animate alongside other people, by vibe. Every group below is real and active, from [the directory's creative & art page](/creative-art/).

## If you want cozy and low-pressure

- **[Gerrard Art Space](/creative-art/#gerrard-art-space)** — a community gallery and workshop space in Little India with drop-in life drawing and classes.
- **[Don Valley Art Club](/creative-art/#don-valley-art-club)** — a long-running painting club with workshops, drop-in life drawing and member shows.
- **[East End Arts](/creative-art/#east-end-arts)** — a community arts organization running classes and workshops out of St. Matthew's Clubhouse on Broadview.
- **[Toronto School of Art](/creative-art/#toronto-school-of-art)** — an independent art school near Queen West with drop-in classes, life drawing and open studios.
- **[CreativeMornings Toronto](/creative-art/#creativemornings-toronto)** — free monthly morning talks for the city's designers, artists and makers.

## If you like to sketch out in the world

- **[Urban Sketchers Toronto](/creative-art/#urban-sketchers-toronto)** — on-location drawing meets in different neighbourhoods, from Allan Gardens to Cabbagetown. All levels welcome.
- **[Toronto Comic Jam](/creative-art/#toronto-comic-jam)** — drawing comics with strangers since 1996, the last Sunday of the month at the back of the Cameron House.
- **[Artists' Network of Riverdale](/creative-art/#artists'-network-of-riverdale)** — a volunteer-run collective behind the annual Riverdale ArtWalk, with shows and drop-in life drawing.
- **[401 Richmond](/creative-art/#401-richmond)** — a heritage arts hub with nearly 150 artists, cultural producers and nonprofits, plus galleries to wander on a Saturday.

## If you want your specific creative community

- **[Open Studio](/creative-art/#open-studio)** — an artist-run printmaking centre at 401 Richmond with workshops and open studio access.
- **[Toronto Animated Image Society (TAIS)](/creative-art/#toronto-animated-image-society-%28tais%29)** — an artist-run centre for independent animation: workshops, equipment and screenings.
- **[Trinity Square Video](/creative-art/#trinity-square-video)** — media art workshops, equipment and exhibitions for emerging video artists.
- **[Tangled Art + Disability](/creative-art/#tangled-art-%2B-disability)** — disability-led exhibitions, residencies and a community for disabled artists and allies.
- **[Workman Arts](/creative-art/#workman-arts)** — arts and mental health workshops, training and membership for artists with lived experience.

## Making the most of it

**You don't need to be good.** Every group above is built for showing up, not showing off. The point is the making and the company.

**Bring the bare minimum and borrow the rest.** A pencil and notebook is enough for a sketch walk or comic jam; studios supply the presses and the model.

**Go a few times.** Like everything in our guide to [making friends in Toronto](/blog/how-to-make-friends-in-toronto/), the magic is repetition — the same faces at the same table until they're your people.
