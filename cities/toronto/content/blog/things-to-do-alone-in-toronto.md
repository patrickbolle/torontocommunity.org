Doing things alone in Toronto is easy. The city is full of solo-shaped hours: a long walk through the Don Valley, a coffee at a counter, a slow browse through a record shop. The harder question, and the one people are usually really asking, is: *what can I do alone that doesn't keep me alone?*

Here's the secret about the activities below: **almost everyone else showed up alone too.** These are the places where being solo isn't just acceptable — it's the default entry ticket, and where going twice makes you a regular. Every link goes to the [directory](/), where you'll find the actual groups, times, and websites.

## Quietly social (no talking required)

Perfect if you want company without conversation — or want the conversation to be optional.

- **Silent Book Club (Toronto)** — bring your own book, read in companionable silence, and chat only if you feel like it. Free, with no assigned book to finish. Find it under [book clubs](/book-clubs/#silent-book-club-(toronto)).
- **Shut Up & Write! Toronto** — quick introductions, a focused writing block, then a progress check-in. Free and open to writers at any stage. Under [writing](/writing/#shut-up-%26-write!-toronto).
- **[Urban Sketchers Toronto](/creative-art/#urban-sketchers-toronto)** — outdoor sketching meetups that move around the city's neighbourhoods. Bring a sketchbook, draw on location, share your work if you want to. All skill levels welcome.
- **[Consciousness Explorers Club](/mindfulness-meditation/#consciousness-explorers-club)** — Monday-night sits at St Stephen-in-the-Fields near Kensington Market, with a playful, pluralist crowd. Drop in and sit; stay for the conversation if you're up for it.
- **[Press Vinyl Cafe](/vinyl-listening-bars/#press-vinyl-cafe)** — pull a record from the bins on the Danforth, order a coffee, and listen. Nobody expects you to talk, but the regulars are happy to.

## Solo by design, social by accident

Activities where you participate as an individual — but the same faces show up every week, and that's how it starts.

- **[Parkdale Roadrunners](/run-clubs/#parkdale-roadrunners)** — hundreds of runners every Tuesday at 7pm, from first-timers to marathoners. Free, drop-in, and rooted in Parkdale's creative scene.
- **[Basecamp Climbing](/climbing/#basecamp-climbing)** — the Queen West gym is bouldering-only in a converted cinema basement, so you need no partner. Strangers trade beta between attempts.
- **[Kotisauna](/sauna-cold-plunge/#kotisauna)** — hosted 90-minute Finnish sauna sessions at Evergreen Brick Works. Heat, cold shower, rest, and the people on the bench beside you. $25 for a first visit.
- **[Löyly Floating Sauna](/sauna-cold-plunge/#l%C3%B6yly-floating-sauna)** — a shared 75-minute session on a sauna moored in the harbour, with an outdoor plunge pool. You sweat and freeze alongside strangers, then leave with sauna friends.
- **[Toronto Outdoor Club](/hiking-outdoors/#toronto-outdoor-club)** — free, member-run hikes, urban walks, cycling and snowshoeing across the GTA, led by volunteers for every pace.
- **[The Dill Pickleball Club](/pickleball/#the-dill-pickleball-club)** — open play in North York rotates you in with new partners, so arriving alone is the standard. $12 open play.
- **[Toronto Cruisers](/cycling/#toronto-cruisers)** — free Wednesday-night social rides from Bloor and Huron, May through August. No membership, no RSVP, lights and music, and a new route every week.

## Solo with a built-in reason to talk

Formats that hand you conversation, so you don't have to manufacture it.

- **[TorontoBabel](/language-exchange/#torontobabel)** — the whole format is talking to strangers, kindly, in whatever language you're learning. All levels welcome, and one of the most newcomer-friendly rooms in the city.
- **[Board Games and Social](/board-games/#board-games-and-social)** — Friday nights at 30 Leeds St, where regulars teach the rules and the group heads to a pub afterward. Mostly 20s–40s, and easy to join solo.
- **[Quizmaster Jess](/pub-trivia/#quizmaster-jess)** — free general-knowledge trivia across a rotating list of Toronto bars. Tell the host you're solo and you'll be adopted by a team before round one.
- **[Chess in the Park](/chess/#chess-in-the-park)** — free weekend chess outdoors, moving around the city's parks and indoors when it gets cold. Sit down, play, talk.
- **[Practical Philosophy Toronto](/philosophy-intellectual/#practical-philosophy-toronto)** — small groups of six to eight work through one topic a week, Wednesdays and Thursdays at 7:30pm. Free, and no philosophy background needed.
- **[Toronto Contra Dance](/dance/#toronto-contra-dance)** — live musicians and callers who walk you through every dance. No experience or partner required, second and fourth Saturdays at St. Barnabas Hall.

## Solo with your hands busy

- **[ZEI Pottery Studio](/pottery-ceramics/#zei-pottery-studio)** — 1.5-hour Wheel Tasters and longer courses on Queen West, with a wheel each. Clay is a great social lubricant.
- **[Site 3 CoLaboratory](/maker-spaces/#site-3-colaboratory)** — a volunteer-run maker space near Bloor and Ossington with woodworking, metal, electronics and 3D printing. Open house Thursdays 7–10pm.
- **[Toronto Photo Walks](/photography/#toronto-photo-walks)** — the introvert's group activity: together, but behind a lens. Parks, streets and neighbourhoods, with a wide mix of abilities.
- **[Tranzac Open Stage](/music-open-mic/#tranzac-open-stage)** — Monday nights in the Annex, sign-up at 6pm for an eight-minute slot or two songs. Deliberately welcoming to first-timers.
- **[Daily Bread Food Bank](/volunteer/#daily-bread-food-bank)** — weekday shifts sorting and repacking food in South Etobicoke. A shift gives you a role, a task and teammates — no small talk required to start.

## A few notes on going solo

**Solo beats duo for meeting people.** A pair reads as "closed"; a person alone reads as "open." Regulars will talk to you *because* you're alone.

**Pick recurring over one-off.** A one-off event alone is still a one-off event alone. A weekly thing alone becomes, by week three, a weekly thing with people who know your name. (More on that in [how to make friends in Toronto](/blog/how-to-make-friends-in-toronto/).)

**The after-thing is the real thing.** When the run or the sit or the game ends and someone says "we usually grab food" — go. That's where regulars become friends.
