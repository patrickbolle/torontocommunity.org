Pub trivia is a small miracle of a social format. You get a reason to be somewhere, a team to belong to, two hours of low-stakes competition, and the specific joy of one person at the table inexplicably knowing every capital city in West Africa. It's also one of the easiest ways to meet people in Toronto — teams are always looking for one more brain.

Here's where to play across the city. Every listing below is a real, currently running night, and the [pub trivia page](/pub-trivia/) is where we keep the running list.

## The regular circuit

- **[The Rhino](https://www.therhino.ca/trivia)** runs trivia **Mondays at 8pm** at 1249 Queen Street West in Parkdale — a proper west-end pub quiz at a Parkdale institution. Follow @rhinobartoronto for schedule changes.
- **Are You Smarter Than a Scottish Guy?** is the Monday quiz at Brazen Head in Liberty Village (165 East Liberty Street), 7–9pm, hosted by a Scottish host who keeps the room laughing between rounds. It's the night to pick if you like your trivia loud.
- **[Quizmaster Jess](https://www.quizmasterjess.com/trivia-locations)** hosts general-knowledge trivia **Tuesdays at Local Public Eatery Leaside** (180 Laird Drive, doors 6pm, first question 7pm) and **Wednesdays at The Drake Hotel** (1150 Queen Street West). Her team also runs themed nights at Cassette at Gladstone House, Black Sheep in Liberty Village, Eastbound Brewing in Riverside and Brunswick Bierworks in East York.
- **[Almost Jeopardy at Sidebar](https://danforthsidebar.com/)** is the Danforth's Tuesday-night quiz, starting at 7:30pm. It's a sit-down, reserve-a-table kind of night rather than a shouting match.

## Free and niche

- **Free general trivia** is the best-value entry point. Quizmaster Jess's general-knowledge nights cost nothing to play — you just reserve a table ahead of time. First prize at Local Leaside is a $100 gift card.
- **PubStumpers** is a free, drop-in league running at pubs across the city, including the Duke of Kent at 2315 Yonge Street on Tuesdays. Teams play 12-week seasons and the league tracks the winners, which is a nice excuse to become a regular.
- **Horror and cult trivia** lives at [Storm Crow Manor](https://www.stormcrow.com/events) (580 Church Street), where Rue Morgue Horror Trivia runs the last Wednesday of the month at 9pm for $12. It's the most committed crowd in the city, in the best possible way.
- **Question Everything Trivia** at [Insomnia Restaurant](https://www.insomniarestaurant.com/qe-trivia-wednesdays-sundays) (563 Bloor Street West) runs **Wednesdays and Sundays**, hosted by comedian Ari Issac and a rotating cast. It's a loose, funny, pop-culture-heavy quiz — good if you'd rather laugh than keep score.

## For the weekend crowd

- **[Pizza Rustica](https://www.eventbrite.com/e/saturday-night-trivia-in-torontosinglescoupleswelcome-tickets-1222760903779)** at 37 Blue Jays Way hosts Adventure Trivia **every Saturday at 7:30pm**. It's team-based general knowledge, and it's the rare Saturday-night option if weeknights don't work for you.
- **Sunday** belongs to Insomnia on Bloor, which gives the weekend a second chance if Saturday fills up.

## How to actually use trivia to meet people

**Show up solo and ask the host.** Toronto doesn't have a published solo-matching policy, so the host is your best route in — tell them you're on your own and they'll usually find you a table that's short a player. The Meetup group around the Scottish-guy quiz does the same job before the night starts.

**Pick a night and make it a ritual.** The friendships form when you're the *regulars* — same pub, same night, same rivalry with the team by the dartboard. PubStumpers' 12-week seasons are built for exactly this. That repetition is the whole trick, the same one behind our guide to [making friends in Toronto](/blog/how-to-make-friends-in-toronto/).

**Lean into your one weird specialty.** Everybody's the hero of some category eventually. Yours is coming.
