# 🎿 Ski & Snowboard Clubs in Toronto

## High Park Ski Club
- **What:** Toronto's longest-running adult ski and snowboard club, and one of its biggest. Coach day trips most winter weekends, free lessons from certified instructors, and a full slate of longer trips across Canada and abroad. A busy year-round social calendar keeps the same crew coming back
- **Where:** Pick-ups across the city, including High Park (180 Colborne Lodge Dr)
- **Find it:** [hpsc.ca](https://hpsc.ca/)

## North Toronto Ski Club
- **What:** A travelling club for alpine skiers, snowboarders and cross-country skiers, run entirely by member volunteers. Saturday, Sunday and Wednesday day trips to Ontario hills, longer trips further afield, and off-season socials. Three city pick-up points make it easy to join without a car
- **Where:** Merton Street pick-up, Yonge & Davisville
- **When:** Sat, Sun & Wed day trips
- **Find it:** [northtorontoskiclub.wildapricot.org](https://northtorontoskiclub.wildapricot.org/)

## Toronto Ski Hawks
- **What:** Volunteer-run club teaching alpine skiing to people who are blind or partially sighted, going since 1969 and the first club of its kind in Canada. Guides ski alongside each athlete for nine weeks every winter at Ontario private and semi-private clubs
- **Where:** Toronto and the Collingwood area
- **Find it:** [torontoskihawks.org](http://www.torontoskihawks.org/)

## Five Winds Backcountry Ski & Snowshoe Club
- **What:** A GTA wilderness club that has been running for more than 50 years. Sunday coach buses head out for all-day backcountry cross-country skiing and snowshoeing, split into groups by skill level, often into the Muskoka and Gibson River forests. A year-round club with trips in the other seasons too
- **When:** Sundays
- **Find it:** [skifivewinds.wildapricot.org](https://skifivewinds.wildapricot.org/)

## Alpine Club of Canada. Toronto Section
- **What:** The Toronto wing of the national alpine club. Members backcountry ski, cross-country ski and ski tour, alongside ice climbing, mountaineering and hiking trips. A good fit if you like your turns earned and want partners who take snow safety seriously
- **Find it:** [alpineclubofcanada.ca/section/toronto](https://alpineclubofcanada.ca/section/toronto/)

## University of Toronto Ski & Snowboard Club
- **What:** U of T's biggest winter sports club, open to students. Weekly Blue Mountain day trips through the season, a reading-week trip to Mont Tremblant, and bigger western trips to Revelstoke, Panorama and Banff. Socials and a mentorship program run alongside the skiing
- **Where:** University of Toronto, St George campus
- **Find it:** [sop.utoronto.ca/group/ski-and-snowboard-club](https://sop.utoronto.ca/group/ski-and-snowboard-club/)

## Ontario Track3 Adaptive Sports Association
- **What:** Volunteer-driven charity that teaches skiing and snowboarding to people with physical, cognitive and developmental disabilities. Instructors train in adaptive techniques, and trained volunteers work alongside each athlete. New volunteers are welcomed every season
- **Find it:** [track3.org](https://www.track3.org/)

## Toronto Ski Club
- **What:** Private club with its own clubhouse at the base of Blue Mountain. Midweek and full-season memberships, coed and women's-only adult ski groups, and a lively après-ski and social calendar. A way to ski the same hill every weekend with the same people
- **Where:** Blue Mountain, Collingwood
- **Find it:** [torontoskiclub.on.ca](https://www.torontoskiclub.on.ca/)

## Venues & Resources

## Earl Bales Ski & Snowboard Centre
- **What:** The City of Toronto's own ski hill in North York, with beginner and intermediate runs, rentals and city-run lessons for kids and adults. Inexpensive lift tickets and night skiing make it the easiest place in the city to learn
- **Where:** Earl Bales Park, North York
- **Find it:** [toronto.ca, downhill ski & snowboard centres](https://www.toronto.ca/explore-enjoy/parks-recreation/program-activities/ice-snow-activities/downhill-skiing-snowboarding-centres/)

## Blue Mountain
- **What:** Ontario's biggest resort and the default weekend mountain for Toronto skiers, dozens of runs, terrain parks, night skiing, and a village of bars and restaurants at the bottom. Where a lot of GTA bus trips end up
- **Where:** Collingwood
- **Find it:** [bluemountain.ca](https://www.bluemountain.ca/)

## Chicopee Ski & Summer Resort
- **What:** Community ski hill in Kitchener with beginner and intermediate runs, a terrain park, a snow school and night skiing. A cheaper, quieter alternative to Blue Mountain and a favourite for families learning together
- **Where:** Kitchener
- **Find it:** [chicopee.ca](https://www.chicopee.ca/)
