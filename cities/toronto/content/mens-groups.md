# 👔 Men's Groups in Toronto & the GTA

## Toronto & York Region Men's Circles — MDI
- **What:** Weekly men's teams where you set goals, report back, and talk honestly about work, relationships and fatherhood. Part of Mentor Discover Inspire, with teams across the city and a 537-member Meetup group.
- **Where:** Downtown Toronto, East York, Etobicoke and York Region
- **When:** Multiple nights weekly
- **Cost:** Free to try
- **Find it:** [meetup.com/toronto-gta-mens-teams-meetings](https://www.meetup.com/toronto-gta-mens-teams-meetings/)

## ManKind Project Toronto Men's Circles
- **What:** Peer-run men's circles in the ManKind Project tradition, open to visitors whether or not they've completed the New Warrior Training Adventure. The Toronto I-Group meets weekly, and the Catacomb Warriors circle meets every other Thursday near Danforth & Broadview.
- **Where:** 15 Iceboat Terrace and Danforth & Broadview
- **When:** Mondays 7pm; alternate Thursdays 6:30pm
- **Find it:** [mankindproject.ca/ontario-mens-groups](https://www.mankindproject.ca/ontario-mens-groups)

## Canadian Centre for Men and Families
- **What:** A Toronto hub for the health and well-being of boys, men, fathers and families since 2014. Runs a men's peer support group, counselling, fathering programs, a legal clinic and support for male survivors in an open, inclusive space.
- **Where:** 26 Mansur Terrace, North York
- **Find it:** [menandfamilies.org/centres-orig/toronto](https://menandfamilies.org/centres-orig/toronto/)

## Talk It Out — Black Men's Mental Health
- **What:** The Sunday Dinner Series, a quarterly facilitated peer discussion group for Black men across the Greater Toronto Area. Run by Talk It Out with Black Daddies Club and student-clinicians with shared lived experience, covering mental, emotional and relational well-being.
- **Where:** Across the GTA
- **When:** Quarterly Sunday dinners
- **Find it:** [talkitoutto.ca/black-mens-mental-health](https://talkitoutto.ca/black-mens-mental-health/)

## Black Daddies Club
- **What:** A long-running Toronto community for Black fathers and Black men, built around Sunday Dinners, intergenerational conversations and programs on fatherhood, identity, vulnerability and mental health.
- **Where:** Toronto and the GTA
- **Find it:** [theblackdaddiesclub.com](https://theblackdaddiesclub.com/)

## Prime Timers Toronto
- **What:** A social group for older gay, bi and trans men (40+), with coffees, lunches, parties, speakers and a monthly euchre afternoon. Guest-friendly socials in The 519 ballroom, with a genial room of people who share similar experiences.
- **Where:** The 519, 519 Church Street
- **When:** Third Saturday monthly
- **Find it:** [the519.org/programs/prime-timers](https://www.the519.org/programs/prime-timers/)

## Toronto Dads Group
- **What:** A 737-member community of dads — married, single, gay, trans, adoptive, step, stay-at-home and working — who meet several times a month with their kids at parks, playgrounds and museums, plus Dad's Nights Out. No dues, no attendance expectations.
- **Where:** Parks and venues across Toronto and the GTA
- **When:** Several meetups a month
- **Cost:** No group dues
- **Find it:** [meetup.com/torontodadsgroup](https://www.meetup.com/torontodadsgroup/)

## Fairlawn Avenue Men's Group
- **What:** A monthly men's group at Fairlawn Avenue United Church for friendship and wide-ranging discussion — a low-key room to talk about whatever the group wants to dig into.
- **Where:** 28 Fairlawn Avenue, North York
- **When:** First Monday morning, Sept–June
- **Find it:** [fairlawnchurch.ca/events/tag/mens-group](https://www.fairlawnchurch.ca/events/tag/mens-group/)

## Venues & Resources

## The 519
- **What:** The 2SLGBTQ+ community centre at Church and Wellesley, and the home of Prime Timers socials and other groups for gay, bi and trans men. Drop in to see what's on.
- **Find it:** [the519.org](https://www.the519.org/)

## Native Canadian Centre of Toronto
- **What:** Indigenous cultural centre offering men's and women's circles, teaching circles, visiting elders and community health support. A place to connect with culture and other men.
- **Find it:** [ncct.on.ca](https://ncct.on.ca/)
