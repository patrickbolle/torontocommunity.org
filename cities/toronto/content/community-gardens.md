# 🌱 Community Gardens in Toronto

## Black Creek Community Farm
- **What:** Eight-acre urban farm at Jane & Steeles, growing food and running hands-on programs for one of Canada's most densely populated neighbourhoods. Volunteer days, community garden plots and a Thursday farm stand make it easy to get involved
- **Where:** 4929 Jane St, North York
- **Cost:** Free to visit
- **Find it:** [blackcreekfarm.ca](https://blackcreekfarm.ca/en/support-us/volunteer-with-us)

## The Stop Community Food Centre
- **What:** Community food centre whose Green Barn holds a greenhouse, a sheltered garden and community gardens, with partner gardens across the city. Volunteer shifts, seedling giveaways and a year-round farmers' market
- **Where:** Green Barn, 601 Christie St
- **Find it:** [thestop.org/programs/urban-agriculture](https://thestop.org/programs/urban-agriculture/)

## Not Far From The Tree
- **What:** Volunteer fruit-picking project rescuing overlooked Toronto fruit since 2008. Join a pick, meet neighbours and send a third of the harvest to community food groups
- **Where:** City-wide
- **Find it:** [notfarfromthetree.org](https://notfarfromthetree.org/)

## Friends of Allan Gardens
- **What:** Volunteer group caring for Allan Gardens and its conservatory, including a new teaching garden. New gardening volunteers join a waitlist, so apply early
- **Where:** Allan Gardens, Carlton & Jarvis
- **Find it:** [friendsofallangardens.ca/volunteer](https://www.friendsofallangardens.ca/volunteer)

## Green Thumbs Growing Kids
- **What:** Food and nature literacy charity running school gardens and the Green Thumbs UP rooftop farm in Regent Park. Volunteer sessions in the rooftop garden run about 1.5 hours
- **Where:** 585 Dundas St E, Regent Park
- **Find it:** [greenthumbsto.org/volunteering](https://www.greenthumbsto.org/volunteering)

## The Common Table Farm
- **What:** Urban farm on land shared by Church of Our Saviour, growing culturally relevant produce for Flemingdon Park families and seniors since 2017. Youth farmer and volunteer opportunities
- **Where:** 1 Laurentide Dr, North York
- **Find it:** [flemingdonparkministry.com/the-common-table-farm-2](https://flemingdonparkministry.com/the-common-table-farm-2/)

## Etobicoke Horticultural Society
- **What:** Garden society marking a century of growing in Etobicoke. Monthly speaker meetings with a flower show, raffle and plant sales, in person and on Zoom
- **Where:** Fairfield Seniors Centre, 80 Lothian Ave, Etobicoke
- **When:** First Wednesday monthly, 7pm
- **Find it:** [etobicokehort.ca/monthly-meetings-1](https://etobicokehort.ca/monthly-meetings-1)

## East York Garden Club
- **What:** "Getting dirty together since 1926" — a friendly club for gardeners of all levels, with guest speakers, plant sales and community beautification projects
- **Where:** Stan Wadlow Clubhouse, 373 Cedarvale Ave
- **When:** Third Monday monthly, 7:30pm
- **Cost:** $25/year individual
- **Find it:** [eastyorkgardenclub.ca](https://eastyorkgardenclub.ca/)

## Horticultural Societies of Parkdale & Toronto
- **What:** The Hort brings Parkdale and Toronto gardeners together for monthly speakers, community garden projects and the Parkdale Seedy Saturday & Eco Fair. Guests are welcome
- **Where:** Bonar-Parkdale Presbyterian Church, 250 Dunn Ave
- **When:** Last Monday monthly, Sept–May
- **Cost:** $5 suggested for guests
- **Find it:** [parkdaletorontohort.com](https://www.parkdaletorontohort.com/)

## Cloverleaf Garden Club of Mississauga
- **What:** Mississauga garden club with expert guest speakers, a plant sale and a friendly room of gardeners happy to answer questions
- **Where:** Mississauga Seniors' Centre, 1389 Cawthra Rd
- **When:** Third Wednesday monthly, Jan–Jun & Sept–Nov, 7pm
- **Find it:** [cloverleafgardenclub.org/meetings-events](https://www.cloverleafgardenclub.org/meetings-events)

## Venues & Resources

### City of Toronto Community Gardens
- **What:** City list of community gardens across Toronto with addresses and districts, plus how the program works. Many gardens have a waiting list
- **Find it:** [toronto.ca/community-gardens](https://www.toronto.ca/explore-enjoy/parks-recreation/places-spaces/beaches-gardens-attractions/gardens-and-horticulture/community-gardens/)

### Toronto Urban Growers
- **What:** City-wide network with a map of urban farms and gardens, plus an urban agriculture directory and get-involved listings
- **Find it:** [torontourbangrowers.org](https://torontourbangrowers.org/)
