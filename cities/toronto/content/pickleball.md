# 🏓 Pickleball Clubs & Leagues in Toronto

## Toronto Pickleball Club
- **What:** A member-run club playing indoor sessions at Leaside High School, with a Blue/Purple rating system, socials and a winter house league where you can sign up solo and get placed on a team. Built around community and friendly competition
- **Where:** Leaside
- **When:** Sundays
- **Cost:** $120/season house league
- **Find it:** [torontopickleballclub.ca](https://www.torontopickleballclub.ca/)

## Queer Pickleball Club
- **What:** A not-for-profit collective for queer women, trans and non-binary players aged 19+, with drop-ins at Parkdale rec centres, beginner coaching and a pay-it-forward fund. Membership is required, and the vibe is deliberately low-pressure and inclusive
- **Where:** Parkdale
- **When:** Sundays & Tuesdays
- **Cost:** $8 drop-in
- **Find it:** [queerpickleballclub.ca](https://queerpickleballclub.ca/)

## The Dill Pickleball Club
- **What:** An indoor club in North York with five courts, open-play sessions that rotate you in with new partners, a Road to 3.0 skill-builder league and coaching. No partner needed, and all levels are welcome
- **Where:** North York
- **Cost:** $12 open play
- **Find it:** [thedillpickleballclub.com](https://thedillpickleballclub.com/)

## Pickleplex Downsview
- **What:** A 13-court indoor pickleball club at Downsview Park with open play, leagues, lessons and court bookings. Its leagues and socials pull players from across North York and Vaughan, so there's usually a game at your level
- **Where:** Downsview, North York
- **When:** Open daily 8am, 11pm
- **Find it:** [pickleplex.ca/locations/downsview](https://pickleplex.ca/locations/downsview/)

## EA Pickleball
- **What:** A non-profit York Region club running a development doubles league where you're assigned new partners each week and ranked on individual points. No partner needed, with lessons, clinics and tournaments alongside the league
- **Where:** Newmarket & York Region
- **When:** Weekly leagues
- **Find it:** [pickleballcanada.org/club/ea-pickleball](https://secure.pickleballcanada.org/club/ea-pickleball/33103/)

## Markham Pickleball Club
- **What:** A non-profit club founded in 2021 with indoor play through the winter, beginner clinics, a club championship and outdoor courts that open in May. One of Ontario's most active and inclusive pickleball communities
- **Where:** Markham
- **Find it:** [pickleballcanada.org/club/markham-pickleball-club](https://secure.pickleballcanada.org/club/markham-pickleball-club/32000/)

## Richmond Hill Pickleball Club
- **What:** An all-level club playing out of Rouge Woods Community Centre and the Richmond Green courts, with meet-ups, round-robin play, ladders, leagues and tournaments. Open to anyone who wants to learn or play
- **Where:** Richmond Hill
- **Find it:** [pickleballcanada.org/club/richmond-hill-pickleball-club](https://secure.pickleballcanada.org/club/richmond-hill-pickleball-club/33049/)

## Oakville Pickleball Association
- **What:** A non-profit, membership-based club with weekly play sorted by level, novice Tuesdays, all-level Wednesdays, intermediate Fridays and competitive Saturdays, plus leagues, clinics and socials. Welcoming to newcomers
- **Where:** Oakville
- **When:** Tuesdays, Saturdays
- **Find it:** [oakvillepickleball.org](https://oakvillepickleball.org/)

## Venues & Spaces

## 6ix Pickle
- **What:** Downtown's only dedicated indoor pickleball club, open 24/7 with four courts, open-play sessions, league nights, a pro shop and a bar. Social play is the draw here, there's even a Dink 'N' Rave night
- **Where:** Downtown (Berkeley St)
- **Cost:** Open play $15-28
- **Find it:** [6ixpickle.com](https://6ixpickle.com/)

## The Picklr Toronto West
- **What:** A nine-court indoor club in Etobicoke and home base for Toronto United Pickleball Club, the city's CNPL team. Open play, leagues, clinics and socials under one membership
- **Where:** Etobicoke
- **Cost:** Memberships from $89/month
- **Find it:** [thepicklr.ca/locations/toronto-west](https://thepicklr.ca/locations/toronto-west/)

## Vaughan Pickleball
- **What:** Eleven indoor courts at Keele and Langstaff with leagues, ladders, clinics, tournaments and member play. A family-run facility that has been hosting GTA players for years
- **Where:** Concord, Vaughan
- **Find it:** [vaughanpickleball.com](https://vaughanpickleball.com/)

## City of Toronto Pickleball Courts
- **What:** The city's official court list, more than 300 outdoor pickleball courts across 108 locations, plus dozens of indoor drop-in sites at community centres. Free, first-come outdoor play all summer
- **Where:** Across Toronto
- **Cost:** Free outdoor courts
- **Find it:** [toronto.ca/pickleball-court-listings](https://www.toronto.ca/explore-enjoy/parks-recreation/places-spaces/parks-and-recreation-facilities/pickleball-court-listings/)
