# 🥾 Hiking & Outdoors in Toronto

## Toronto Outdoor Club
- **What:** Free, member-run outdoor club with hikes, urban walks, cycling, canoeing and snowshoeing across the GTA. Volunteers lead trips for every pace
- **Cost:** Free to join
- **Find it:** [torontooutdoorclub.com](https://www.torontooutdoorclub.com/)

## Toronto Bruce Trail Club
- **What:** The largest of the nine Bruce Trail Conservancy clubs, running guided hikes, urban walks and trail-maintenance days on the Toronto section of the Bruce Trail
- **Find it:** [torontobrucetrailclub.org](https://torontobrucetrailclub.org/)

## Alpine Club of Canada — Toronto Section
- **What:** Toronto section of Canada's national mountaineering club: hiking, climbing, backcountry skiing, socials and courses for all levels
- **Find it:** [alpineclubtoronto.ca](https://alpineclubtoronto.ca/)

## Wilderness Union
- **What:** Toronto outdoor community running day hikes, canoe trips and backcountry courses, plus member-led run, climbing and book clubs
- **Find it:** [wildernessunion.com](https://www.wildernessunion.com/)

## Toronto Trail Runners
- **What:** Free weekly trail runs through the Don Valley and Toronto's ravines, plus group runs for every pace
- **Cost:** Free
- **Find it:** [torontotrailrunners.ca](https://www.torontotrailrunners.ca/)

## Toronto Field Naturalists
- **What:** Naturalist club leading bird walks, nature walks and field trips across the GTA since 1923
- **Find it:** [torontofieldnaturalists.org](https://torontofieldnaturalists.org/)

## High Park Nature Centre
- **What:** Community nature centre in High Park with guided hikes, nature clubs for kids and adults, and workshops
- **Where:** High Park
- **Find it:** [highparknaturecentre.com](https://highparknaturecentre.com/)

## University of Toronto Outdoors Club (UTOC)
- **What:** Student-run outdoors club open to everyone, with hiking, camping, canoeing and cabin trips in Ontario's parks
- **Find it:** [utoc.ca](https://utoc.ca/)

## Outdoor Club of East York
- **What:** Neighbourhood outdoor club running group hikes, walks and cross-country ski outings in and beyond the GTA
- **Where:** East York
- **Find it:** [outdoorclubofeastyork.wildapricot.org](https://outdoorclubofeastyork.wildapricot.org/)

## Five Winds Backcountry Ski & Snowshoe Club
- **What:** GTA-based wilderness club running Sunday coach-bus trips for backcountry cross-country skiing and snowshoeing, grouped by skill
- **When:** Sundays in winter
- **Find it:** [skifivewinds.wildapricot.org](https://skifivewinds.wildapricot.org/)

## Colour the Trails
- **What:** BIPOC-focused outdoor community organizing accessible hikes, camping and snow sports, with regular Ontario and Toronto outings
- **Find it:** [colourthetrails.com](https://colourthetrails.com/)

## Let's Hike T.O.
- **What:** Non-profit hiking community welcoming people of colour, newcomers and young adults with low-cost, led group hikes across the GTA
- **Find it:** [letshiketo.ca](https://www.letshiketo.ca/)

## Wilde Outdoors & Adventures
- **What:** Year-round outdoor adventures for gay men and allies — hiking, kayaking, cycling and snowshoeing in and around the GTA
- **Find it:** [meetup.com/wilde-outdoors-adventures-for-gay-men-allies](https://www.meetup.com/wilde-outdoors-adventures-for-gay-men-allies/)

## GTA Girls Hike Club
- **What:** Women's hiking community redefining adventure at any age and pace — guided group hikes and outdoor meetups across the GTA
- **Find it:** [@gtagirlshikeclub](https://www.instagram.com/gtagirlshikeclub/)

## Toronto Hiking Moms
- **What:** Non-profit hiking group for new mothers and parents — easy-paced, companionable hikes and meetups to get outdoors with little ones
- **Find it:** [eventbrite.ca/o/toronto-hiking-moms](https://www.eventbrite.ca/o/toronto-hiking-moms-86853910113)

## Venues & Resources

## Rouge National Urban Park
- **What:** Canada's largest urban park — 12 km of trails and free Parks Canada guided walks year-round
- **Find it:** [parks.canada.ca/pn-np/on/rouge](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/on/rouge)

## Evergreen Brick Works
- **What:** Nature hub in the Don Valley with guided walks, nature programs and community events
- **Find it:** [evergreen.ca](https://www.evergreen.ca/)
