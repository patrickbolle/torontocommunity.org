# 🎙️ Karaoke Bars & Nights in Toronto

## Toronto Karaoke Meetup Group
- **What:** A big, friendly Meetup crew for people who love a stage, run by longtime host KJ Sharon. She reserves a table near the mic, makes introductions and runs icebreakers, so showing up solo is normal. Regulars sing Thursdays at The Irish Rose and Fridays at Scruffy Murphy's.
- **Where:** The Irish Rose Pub (1095 Weston Rd) and Scruffy Murphy's (225 The East Mall, Etobicoke)
- **When:** Thursdays & Fridays 9pm
- **Find it:** [meetup.com/toronto-karaoke-meetup-group](https://www.meetup.com/toronto-karaoke-meetup-group/)

## Amuse Bouche Karaoke at Three Dollar Bill
- **What:** Sunday-night queer karaoke at a Queen West bar, with guest hosts and a room that treats first-timers like regulars. Birthday groups, duets and full-room singalongs are all part of the ritual.
- **Where:** Three Dollar Bill, 1592 Queen St W
- **When:** Sundays
- **Find it:** [threedollarbill.ca/amuse-bouche-karaoke](https://www.threedollarbill.ca/amuse-bouche-karaoke)

## Cassette Karaoke
- **What:** Weekly Saturday karaoke in the Gladstone House bar, hosted by Question Everything, free, low-pressure, and a crowd that knows every word. Once a month the mic goes live-band with Good Enough Live Karaoke.
- **Where:** Cassette, 1214 Queen St W
- **When:** Saturdays 8pm; live band monthly
- **Cost:** No cover
- **Find it:** [cassetteto.com/whats-on](https://www.cassetteto.com/whats-on)

## Dina's Tavern Karaoke
- **What:** A Spadina tavern deep in the local music scene, with karaoke several nights a week. Thursday nights with Ivan Rivers, live-band karaoke and guest-hosted parties. Affordable drinks, low attitude and plenty of regulars.
- **Where:** 486 Spadina Ave
- **When:** Several nights weekly
- **Find it:** [dinastavern.com](https://www.dinastavern.com/)

## Saturday Karaoke at The Lodge
- **What:** Free Saturday karaoke upstairs at O'Grady's, hosted by Brayo Bryans until 2am. A queer-friendly Church Street room with big anthems, late-night courage and a proper after-party crowd.
- **Where:** The Lodge, 518 Church St (upstairs at O'Grady's)
- **When:** Saturdays 10pm, 2am
- **Cost:** Free
- **Find it:** [eventbrite.com. Saturday Karaoke at The Lodge](https://www.eventbrite.com/e/saturday-karaoke-with-brayo-bryans-at-the-lodge-tickets-2002610394599)

## Venues & Spaces

## Bar+ Karaoke Lounge
- **What:** Downtown private-room karaoke at Yonge & Dundas, with tablet song selection, a huge multilingual library and a photobooth. Rooms fit small groups up to parties of 30, so it's easy to bring the whole crew.
- **Where:** 360 Yonge St
- **Find it:** [bar-plus.com](https://www.bar-plus.com/)

## Boom Karaoke
- **What:** A sleek basement karaoke bar in Church-Wellesley with private rooms, 150,000+ songs and a proper cocktail list. The newest room in the neighbourhood, built for groups who want the mic to themselves.
- **Where:** 577 Yonge St
- **Find it:** [boomkaraoke.ca](https://www.boomkaraoke.ca/)

## SU Karaoke Bar
- **What:** Private karaoke rooms on Yonge near Church, open late seven days a week with a big multilingual songbook and a full drink menu. An easy, unstuffy spot for a group night.
- **Where:** 649 Yonge St
- **Find it:** [sukaraoke.com](https://www.sukaraoke.com/)
