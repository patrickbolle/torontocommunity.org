# 🎵 Open Mics & Music Communities in Toronto

## Tranzac Open Stage
- **What:** Monday-night open stage in the Southern Cross room of the Tranzac, a member-supported music and arts club. Sign up at 6pm for an eight-minute slot (or two songs); the room is deliberately welcoming to first-timers, and the club's calendar also carries bluegrass jams and the Saturday afternoon Jamzac.
- **Where:** 292 Brunswick Avenue, The Annex
- **When:** Mondays 6:30pm
- **Find it:** [tranzac.org](https://tranzac.org/)

## Open Mic Mondays at The Painted Lady
- **What:** Long-running Monday open mic hosted by Kunle in a live-music bar on the Ossington strip. Sign-up is in person at 8pm, a feature artist plays 8:15–9pm, then the open mic runs 9pm–2am with two songs per performer. Come early, stay late, cheer loudly.
- **Where:** Ossington Strip
- **When:** Mondays 9pm
- **Find it:** [thepaintedlady.ca](https://www.thepaintedlady.ca/)

## Sunday Jam Night at Grossman's Tavern
- **What:** Weekly Sunday jam hosted by Ken Yoshioka at a Spadina tavern that has been a blues and jazz room for decades. Bring an instrument and sit in with a crowd that has heard it all before — in the best way.
- **Where:** 377 Spadina Avenue
- **When:** Sundays 9pm
- **Find it:** [grossmanstavern.com](https://grossmanstavern.com/)

## Only Jazz Jam at The Only Cafe
- **What:** A pay-what-you-can jazz jam in the back cocktail bar of a Danforth café, alongside a monthly poetry and open mic night. The room is small, the regulars are friendly, and the focus is on playing rather than performing.
- **Where:** 966 Danforth Avenue
- **Cost:** Pay-what-you-can
- **Find it:** [theonlycafe.com](https://www.theonlycafe.com/)

## Franc'Open Mic at Free Times Cafe
- **What:** Toronto's first French-language open mic, open to song, poetry, comedy and anything else you can do on a mic. Held in the back room of a longtime College Street café; sign-up is at 7:30pm and the show starts at 8pm.
- **Where:** 320 College Street
- **Find it:** [freetimescafe.com](https://www.freetimescafe.com/)

## JazzInToronto Late Night Jam
- **What:** JazzInToronto's Saturday-night jam at the Emmet Ray, with an open sign-up for players at every level. Free for jammers, pay-what-you-can for listeners, 11:30pm–2am. The same organization publishes the city's jazz gig listings and runs community shows.
- **Where:** The Emmet Ray, 924 College Street
- **When:** Saturdays 11:30pm
- **Cost:** Free for jammers, PWYC for listeners
- **Find it:** [jazzintoronto.ca](https://jazzintoronto.ca/)

## Toronto Songwriters Association
- **What:** A monthly songwriting circle where members bring new songs for constructive feedback, work through themed assignments and hear what everyone else has been writing. Low-key, useful and full of people who take songs seriously without taking themselves too seriously.
- **Find it:** [torontosongwriters.com](https://www.torontosongwriters.com/)

## Kensington Unplugged
- **What:** A weekly Nashville-style writers' round — four songwriters an hour, each playing original songs and telling the stories behind them. Curated rather than open-mic, which keeps the listening room quiet and the songwriting sharp.
- **Where:** Supermarket, 268 Augusta Avenue, Kensington Market
- **When:** Tuesdays 7–11pm
- **Cost:** Free
- **Find it:** [instagram.com/kensingtonunplugged](https://www.instagram.com/kensingtonunplugged/) | [Supermarket](https://www.supermarkettoronto.com/)

## Corktown Ukulele Jam
- **What:** Toronto's ukulele institution, running since 2009: an 8pm workshop, then a group strum and open stage in the Ukulele Lounge. Beginners are genuinely welcome, and the regulars are the reason the room keeps filling.
- **Where:** Dominion on Queen, 500 Queen Street East
- **When:** Wednesdays 8–11pm
- **Cost:** $6 workshop; group jam and open stage free
- **Find it:** [torontoukes.wixsite.com](https://torontoukes.wixsite.com/torontoukes/faq)

## Toronto Guitar School
- **What:** A music school that functions as a community hub, with rock-band open jams, ukulele jams, workshops, student concerts and recording nights. A practical place for beginners and returning players to get out of the bedroom and play with other people.
- **Where:** 116 Bond Street, Downtown
- **Find it:** [torontoguitarschool.com](https://www.torontoguitarschool.com/)

## Venues & Spaces

## The Rex Hotel Jazz & Blues Bar
- **What:** A Toronto jazz room since 1951, with live music every day and weekend matinees. The kind of place where local players and lifelong listeners end up at the same table.
- **Where:** 194 Queen Street West
- **When:** Live music daily
- **Find it:** [therex.ca](https://www.therex.ca/)

## Music Gallery
- **What:** Toronto's centre for creative music — experimental, improvised and new music concerts in a warm listening room. A home for the city's stranger, more adventurous musical corners.
- **Where:** 918 Bathurst Street
- **Find it:** [musicgallery.org](https://musicgallery.org/)

## Drom Taberna
- **What:** A late-night Queen West tavern for world music, jazz, brass and flamenco, with a monthly ambient jam (Petal Loop) and a crowd that actually dances. Come for one set and stay for the next three.
- **Where:** 458 Queen Street West
- **Find it:** [dromtaberna.com](https://www.dromtaberna.com/)

## Burdock Brewery
- **What:** A brewery and music hall in Bloordale with a steady calendar of local shows, plus a smaller Kensington Market outpost. Good sound and a neighbourhood feel.
- **Where:** 1184 Bloor Street West
- **Find it:** [burdockbrewery.com](https://burdockbrewery.com/)

## Jazz Bistro
- **What:** An intimate Victoria Street jazz club with live jazz and a proper listening-room feel. A grown-up room where the city's jazz players stretch out.
- **Where:** 251 Victoria Street
- **Find it:** [jazzbistro.ca](https://jazzbistro.ca/)
