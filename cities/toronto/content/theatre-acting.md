# 🎬 Theatre & Acting in Toronto

## Alumnae Theatre Company
- **What:** Founded in 1918 by women graduates of the University of Toronto, this volunteer company stages a full season of plays plus the FireWorks and New Ideas festivals for new work. Members act, direct, design and crew
- **Where:** 70 Berkeley Street, Garden District
- **Find it:** [alumnaetheatre.com](https://alumnaetheatre.com/)

## Village Players
- **What:** Volunteer-run community theatre in the heart of Bloor West Village, putting on five shows a season, from British farce to contemporary drama. Auditions are open to the public and backstage help is always welcome
- **Where:** 2190E Bloor Street West, Bloor West Village
- **Find it:** [villageplayers.net](https://villageplayers.net/)

## Stage Centre Productions
- **What:** Community theatre company in North York staging comedy, drama, mystery and farce at the Fairview Library Theatre. Cast and crew are volunteers, and auditions are open to newcomers
- **Where:** Fairview Library Theatre, 35 Fairview Mall Drive, North York
- **Find it:** [stagecentreproductions.com](https://stagecentreproductions.com/)

## Markham Little Theatre
- **What:** Markham's volunteer community theatre, now in its 60th-anniversary season with a four-show lineup of comedies, mysteries and adventure. Auditions and backstage volunteer calls are posted each season
- **Where:** Markham
- **Find it:** [markhamlittletheatre.com](https://markhamlittletheatre.com/)

## The Oakville Players
- **What:** Community theatre "entertaining audiences since 1962," run entirely by volunteer members who act, direct, build sets and work the box office. New members are welcome on stage and off
- **Where:** Oakville
- **Find it:** [oakvilleplayers.ca](https://oakvilleplayers.ca/)

## Oshawa Little Theatre
- **What:** Community theatre bringing "playful moments to life since 1950," with a mainstage season plus a youth group, summer theatre program and youth orchestra. Volunteers run everything from lights to front of house
- **Where:** 62 Russett Avenue, Oshawa
- **Find it:** [oshawalittletheatre.com](https://oshawalittletheatre.com/)

## No Strings Theatre
- **What:** Toronto music theatre company for young and emerging artists, with a teen intensive for ages 13-17 in acting, singing and dancing, a summer opera intensive, and a workshop series
- **Where:** Toronto
- **Find it:** [nostringstheatre.com](https://nostringstheatre.com/)

## Alexander Showcase Theatre
- **What:** Not-for-profit community theatre producing Broadway musicals, Gilbert & Sullivan, operettas and plays, with a mandate to develop emerging performers and technical crew. Auditions are posted for each production
- **Where:** North York
- **Find it:** [alexandershowcasetheatre.com](https://alexandershowcasetheatre.com/)

## Armstrong Acting Studios
- **What:** Film and TV acting studio in Leslieville offering introductory-to-master classes, self-tape coaching and workshops with working actors. A supportive, inclusive room for people building an acting career
- **Where:** 9 Davies Avenue, Studio 409, Leslieville
- **Find it:** [armstrongactingstudios.com](https://armstrongactingstudios.com/)
