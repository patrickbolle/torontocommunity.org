# 🚴 Cycling Clubs in Toronto

## Toronto Bicycling Network
- **What:** The GTA's largest recreational cycling club, run by volunteers, with urban and country rides for every level — leisurely downtown loops to fast rural centuries. A busy social calendar of pub quizzes, hikes and trips makes it easy to meet people off the bike too
- **Where:** Start points across Toronto
- **When:** Rides almost every day in season
- **Cost:** $50/year
- **Find it:** [tbn.ca](https://www.tbn.ca/)

## Dark Horse Flyers
- **What:** A 300-member adult club riding since 2007, with six levels so newcomers and racers can find the right group. Saturday routes stretch out through the season, and the club is known for being fast but friendly
- **Where:** Meeting points across the city
- **When:** Saturday mornings
- **Find it:** [darkhorseflyers.ca](http://www.darkhorseflyers.ca/)

## LapDogs Cycling Club
- **What:** Multi-discipline club since 2003 — road, mountain, cyclocross, gravel and track — with rides near High Park and in the east end. Women and gender-diverse riders new to the club can request a first-ride chaperone
- **Where:** High Park & the east end
- **Find it:** [lapdogs.ca](https://www.lapdogs.ca/)

## Midweek Cycling Club
- **What:** A learn-to-race club for people who want to try competitive cycling, with coached training rides, a weekly crit series and youth programs. The most welcoming on-ramp to Toronto's race scene
- **Where:** Toronto & Mississauga
- **When:** Monday & Tuesday evenings
- **Find it:** [midweekcyclingclub.ca](https://www.midweekcyclingclub.ca/)

## Beaches Cycling Club
- **What:** A Beaches-based club since 2007 with a safety-first, social culture — road, gravel, mountain and triathlon riders all welcome. New members do a short intro session, then join early-morning rides and coffee stops
- **Where:** Beaches
- **When:** Saturday mornings
- **Cost:** $80/year + Ontario Cycling
- **Find it:** [beachescycling.com](https://beachescycling.com/)

## D'Ornellas Cycling Club
- **What:** A friendly, inclusive club founded by four-time Olympian Eon D'Ornellas, running skills-focused rides from the family bike shop. A good fit if you want coaching and group-riding craft alongside the miles
- **Where:** Scarborough & Richmond Hill
- **When:** Saturday mornings
- **Find it:** [dornellascyclingclub.ca](https://dornellascyclingclub.ca/)

## Etobicoke Cycling Club
- **What:** A west-end club formed in 2014 that rides almost every day at a range of paces, with a no-drop policy and a coffee stop on every ride. Their early-season intro program helps new riders settle in
- **Where:** Etobicoke
- **Find it:** [etobicokecycling.com](http://etobicokecycling.com/)

## Newmarket Eagles Cycling Club
- **What:** A York Region club with organized group rides for most levels, plus time trials, a race team and social events. Rides head out through quiet rural roads north of the city
- **Where:** Newmarket, York Region
- **When:** Weekend mornings
- **Find it:** [newmarketeagles.com](https://www.newmarketeagles.com/)

## Saddle Sisters
- **What:** Morning rides and bikepacking trips for women, trans, femme and non-binary riders. Rides are no-drop, helmets are mandatory, and the calendar is posted on Strava
- **Where:** High Park area
- **Find it:** [saddlesisters.ca](https://saddlesisters.ca/)

## Queer Bike Club TO
- **What:** Biweekly queer rides and events on mixed terrain, open to all levels. No-drop and paced to the slowest rider, with the route and meeting point posted on Instagram
- **Where:** Locations vary
- **When:** Every two weeks
- **Find it:** [instagram.com/queerbikeclubto](https://www.instagram.com/queerbikeclubto/)

## Trailblazers Tandem Cycling Club
- **What:** A registered charity since 1986 that pairs blind, deaf-blind and partially sighted riders with sighted volunteer captains on club-owned tandems. One of the most welcoming communities in Toronto cycling
- **Where:** Toronto
- **When:** Scheduled weekend rides
- **Cost:** $50/year for stokers; free for captains
- **Find it:** [trailblazerstandem.org](https://trailblazerstandem.org/)

## Randonneurs Ontario — Toronto Chapter
- **What:** Non-competitive, self-supported long-distance riding — brevets start at 200 km and run through the countryside on weekends. A small, generous community for riders who like all-day adventures
- **Where:** Starts in Leslieville, Mimico & High Park
- **When:** Weekends
- **Cost:** First ride free, then $40/year
- **Find it:** [randonneursontario.ca](https://www.randonneursontario.ca/calendar)

## Toronto Cruisers
- **What:** Free Wednesday-night social rides with lights and music — a hop-on, hop-off cruise that keeps the group together and changes route every week. No membership, no RSVP, just show up
- **Where:** Bloor St W & Huron St
- **When:** Wednesdays 8pm, May–August
- **Cost:** Free
- **Find it:** [torontocruisers.com](http://torontocruisers.com/)

## Venues & Resources

## Bateman's Bicycle Company
- **What:** Two Toronto shops that double as a ride hub — free Thursday-evening mountain bike rides in the Don Valley split into slow, medium and fast groups, plus road, gravel, women's and kids' rides
- **Where:** 49 Laird Dr & Bathurst St
- **When:** Thursdays 6pm
- **Cost:** Free
- **Find it:** [batemansbikeco.com](https://www.batemansbikeco.com/)

## Urbane Cyclist
- **What:** A worker-owned bike co-op on College St whose Urbane Adventure Club runs easy, no-drop mixed-terrain rides around the city. Rides are announced on Instagram
- **Where:** 280 College St
- **Find it:** [ucycle.com](https://www.ucycle.com/service/urbane-adventure-club/)

## Bikechain
- **What:** A non-profit DIY bike shop at the University of Toronto that runs free, beginner-friendly Tuesday-evening rides at a talking pace. U of T students can borrow a bike for free
- **Where:** University of Toronto
- **When:** Tuesdays, May–September
- **Cost:** Free
- **Find it:** [bikechain.ca](https://bikechain.ca/)
