# 🏺 Pottery / Ceramics in Toronto

## Venues & Spaces

## Toronto Potters
- **What:** Artist-run non-profit collective and studio connecting GTA ceramic artists since 1979. Beginner and intermediate classes (membership not required), workshops, juried exhibitions, studio rentals and a shop of members' work. Volunteer-run and genuinely community-minded
- **Where:** West End
- **Find it:** [torontopotters.com](https://torontopotters.com/)

## The Potter's Studio
- **What:** Non-profit ceramics studio running since 1972, now in a 3,000 sq ft Scarborough space. Adult wheel-throwing and handbuilding classes, drop-in sessions, seasonal pottery sales and take-home kits. A long-running teaching studio with a real neighbourhood following
- **Where:** Scarborough
- **Find it:** [thepottersstudio.ca](https://thepottersstudio.ca/)

## Parkdale Pottery
- **What:** Women-owned teaching studio with taster sessions, 4- and 8-week wheel-throwing and handbuilding courses, sculptural ceramics, kids' and teens' classes, and open-studio membership for students ready to work on their own
- **Where:** Parkdale, 1688 Queen St W
- **Find it:** [parkdalepottery.ca](https://parkdalepottery.ca/)

## ZEI Pottery Studio
- **What:** Two Toronto studios offering 1.5-hour Wheel Tasters, pottery date nights, 4- and 8-week wheel-throwing and handbuilding courses, memberships and private events. Everyone gets their own wheel, beginner-friendly and social
- **Where:** Queen West, 988 Queen St W
- **Find it:** [zeipottery.com](https://zeipottery.com/)

## Clay With Me
- **What:** Two-studio pottery community running wheel-throwing, handbuilding and paint-your-own-pottery sessions, private events, and an Unlimited Creators open-studio membership for independent potters
- **Where:** Roncesvalles
- **Cost:** $250/month open studio
- **Find it:** [claywithme.ca](https://claywithme.ca/)

## Clay Space Studio
- **What:** Leslieville pottery studio with classes, workshops and an open studio space, plus a front window used for rotating art installations. Flexible memberships include wheels, tools, glazes and firing for hobbyists and independent artists
- **Where:** Leslieville
- **Find it:** [clayspacestudio.com](https://clayspacestudio.com/pages/classes)

## Pottery Dream
- **What:** Danforth studio with two- to eight-week wheel-throwing and handbuilding courses, short workshops like the mini pottery wheel, and one-on-one sessions. Welcoming to all skill levels
- **Where:** Danforth, 125 Danforth Ave, 2F
- **Find it:** [potterydream.ca](https://www.potterydream.ca/)

## The Pottery Hut
- **What:** Teaching studio running wheel-throwing, handbuilding, trimming, painting and glazing classes for teens and adults, plus beginner-friendly workshops and PA day and kids' programming
- **Where:** Mississauga, 400 Matheson Blvd E
- **Find it:** [thepotteryhut.ca](https://www.thepotteryhut.ca/)

## Creative Insight Pottery
- **What:** Cozy full-service pottery and art studio with 10-week term classes, one-day wheel classes, drop-ins, workshops, kids' art, and membership and studio rental. Draws potters from across Halton
- **Where:** Waterdown, 23 Main St S
- **Find it:** [creativeinsightpottery.com](https://creativeinsightpottery.com/)

## Burlington Potters' Guild
- **What:** Guild of potters working out of a fully equipped ceramics studio at the Art Gallery of Burlington, earthenware, stoneware, high-fire, reduction, raku and smoke firing, plus a guild library and communal firings. Member-run and deeply community-oriented
- **Where:** Art Gallery of Burlington, 1333 Lakeshore Rd
- **Find it:** [burlingtonpottersguild.com](https://www.burlingtonpottersguild.com/)

## Moj Pottery Studio
- **What:** Markham studio teaching wheel throwing, handbuilding, porcelain and glazing, with painting sessions and group and birthday events. Small and friendly, a good first time at the wheel
- **Where:** Markham, 80 Cathedral High St
- **Find it:** [mojpottery.com](https://mojpottery.com/)
