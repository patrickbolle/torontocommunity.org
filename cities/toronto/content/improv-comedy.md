# 🎭 Improv & Comedy in Toronto

## The Second City Toronto
- **What:** The legendary improv and sketch comedy theatre that launched a generation of Canadian comics, with nightly mainstage shows and a training centre that takes beginners through to advanced performance
- **Where:** 1 York St
- **Find it:** [secondcity.com/toronto](https://www.secondcity.com/toronto/)

## Bad Dog Theatre Company
- **What:** Toronto's home of long-form improv since 1982 — classes in improv, sketch and stand-up plus drop-ins, jams and shows around the city. Warm, playful and genuinely beginner-friendly
- **Where:** 392 Spadina Ave (classes)
- **Find it:** [baddogtheatre.com](https://baddogtheatre.com/)

## Comedy Bar
- **What:** Beloved Bloorcourt comedy venue with two Toronto rooms, hosting improv, sketch and stand-up almost every night — indie teams, drop-in shows and the city's alternative comedy scene
- **Where:** 945 Bloor St W
- **Find it:** [comedybar.ca](https://comedybar.ca/)

## SoCap Comedy (The Social Capital)
- **What:** Danforth comedy hub with up to six shows a night — improv, sketch, stand-up and storytelling — plus an improv school with weekly drop-in classes open to the public
- **Where:** 154 Danforth Ave
- **Find it:** [socap.ca](https://socap.ca/)

## Sweet Action Theatre
- **What:** Intimate 55-seat indie theatre at Youngplace devoted to clown, improv, sketch and puppetry, with affordable classes and a calendar of gleefully strange shows
- **Where:** 180 Shaw St, Unit 106
- **Find it:** [sweetaction.ca](https://www.sweetaction.ca/)

## Knifefight Theatre
- **What:** Parkdale company making comedy-forward theatre and recurring shows — home of Kumite Improv, a monthly six-improviser showdown with live scoring and an audience-fuelled final
- **Where:** The Assembly Theatre, 1479 Queen St W
- **Find it:** [knifefighttheatre.com](https://www.knifefighttheatre.com/)

## The Corner Comedy Club
- **What:** Tiny downtown basement club where the intimacy is the point — nightly stand-up showcases plus a monthly booked open mic for up-and-coming and veteran comics
- **Where:** 243 Queen St W
- **Find it:** [thecornercomedy.com](https://thecornercomedy.com/)

## Backroom Comedy Club
- **What:** Speakeasy-style stand-up room near Christie Pits with pro shows, happy-hour open mics and Monday Night Improv, where new and established acts try out material
- **Where:** 814 Bloor St W, basement unit
- **Find it:** [backroomcomedyclub.com](https://backroomcomedyclub.com/)

## Absolute Comedy Toronto
- **What:** Midtown stand-up club on Yonge Street with touring headliners and a full calendar of shows — a straightforward night out for comedy fans
- **Where:** 2335 Yonge St
- **Find it:** [absolutecomedy.ca/toronto](https://www.absolutecomedy.ca/toronto)

## Improv for New Friends
- **What:** Recurring two-hour beginner improv class and social run by Ralph MacLeod, co-founder of Bad Dog and SoCap — games, scenes and low-pressure mingling for people who want to meet others
- **Where:** SoCap Comedy, 154 Danforth Ave
- **Find it:** [meetup.com/improv-your-social-skills-meetup-group](https://www.meetup.com/improv-your-social-skills-meetup-group/)

## Venues & Spaces

## The Assembly Theatre
- **What:** Parkdale's intimate indie arts space, home to Knifefight's Kumite Improv and a steady run of independent theatre and comedy
- **Where:** 1479 Queen St W
- **Find it:** [theassemblytheatre.com](https://www.theassemblytheatre.com/)

## The Theatre Centre
- **What:** Artist-run performance hub on Queen West with a bar and flexible rooms, hosting independent theatre, comedy and community events
- **Where:** 1115 Queen St W
- **Find it:** [theatrecentre.org](https://www.theatrecentre.org/)

## Rivoli
- **What:** Queen West institution whose backroom hosts sketch, stand-up and comedy nights alongside live music and film
- **Where:** 334 Queen St W
- **Find it:** [rivolitoronto.com](https://www.rivolitoronto.com/)
