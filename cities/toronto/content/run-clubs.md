# 🏃 Run Clubs in Toronto

## Parkdale Roadrunners
- **What:** Toronto's biggest neighbourhood run crew — hundreds of runners every week, from first-timers to marathoners. Free and drop-in, rooted in Parkdale's creative scene
- **Where:** Parkdale
- **When:** Tuesdays 7pm
- **Find it:** [parkdaleroadrunners.com](https://www.parkdaleroadrunners.com/)

## Longboat Roadrunners
- **What:** One of Toronto's oldest clubs (since 1980), coach-led training for everyone from 5K newbies to marathoners. Warm, supportive, all levels welcome
- **Find it:** [longboatroadrunners.com](https://longboatroadrunners.com/)

## Toronto Frontrunners
- **What:** LGBTQ2S+ friendly running and walking group with a no-drop policy, welcoming every pace and identity. Runs year-round, rain or shine
- **Where:** Barbara Hall Park, Church & Wellesley
- **When:** Thursdays & Saturdays
- **Find it:** [frontrunnerstoronto.ca](https://www.frontrunnerstoronto.ca/)

## BlackToe Running Run Club
- **What:** Free weekly group runs from a much-loved local running shop, with 5K and 10K options and pace groups for every level
- **Where:** King West & Midtown
- **When:** Wednesdays 6pm & Sundays 9am
- **Cost:** Free
- **Find it:** [blacktoerunning.com](https://www.blacktoerunning.com/pages/blacktoe-drop-in-runs)

## Culture Athletics Run Club
- **What:** Free community runs from the east-end run + yoga shop, with short, medium and long Sunday routes. Laid-back and welcoming
- **Where:** Riverside (Queen St East)
- **When:** Sundays 8am
- **Cost:** Free
- **Find it:** [cultureathletics.com](https://cultureathletics.com/)

## Midnight Runners Toronto
- **What:** High-energy Tuesday-night "party runs" with music and a big, social crowd. Part of a global run crew, all paces welcome
- **When:** Tuesdays 7pm
- **Find it:** [midnightrunners.com](https://www.midnightrunners.com/cities/toronto)

## Toronto Running Club
- **What:** Friendly midtown club running since 1995 from North Toronto Memorial Community Centre. All levels, from new runners to seasoned marathoners
- **Where:** Midtown (North Toronto)
- **When:** Wednesdays & Saturdays
- **Find it:** [torontorunningclub.com](http://www.torontorunningclub.com/)

## Toronto Olympic Club
- **What:** One of Canada's premier distance-running clubs (since 1954), with coached track and cross-country programs for competitive runners
- **Find it:** [torontoolympicclub.com](https://www.torontoolympicclub.com/)

## Night Terrors Run Crew
- **What:** Late-night running crew for the night owls — community runs after dark, all paces welcome and zero judgement
- **Find it:** [instagram.com/nightterrorsrun](https://www.instagram.com/nightterrorsrun/)

## High Park Rogue Runners
- **What:** Free, inclusive community club running out of High Park — mid-week workouts plus a Sunday long run. Volunteer-run and community-minded
- **Where:** High Park / Bloor West
- **When:** Wednesdays & Fridays 6:30pm · Sundays 8:30am
- **Cost:** Free
- **Find it:** [instagram.com/roguerunners](https://www.instagram.com/roguerunners/)

## Bay Street Run Club
- **What:** After-work runs for the downtown crowd, with 5K and 8K options and friendly pace groups. An easy way to meet people in the core
- **Where:** Financial District
- **When:** Thursdays 6:15pm
- **Find it:** [baystreetrun.club](https://baystreetrun.club/)

## Sauga Valley Run Club
- **What:** 100% volunteer-led Mississauga run club for all paces — roads, trails and good company, with no business behind it
- **Where:** Mississauga
- **When:** Saturdays
- **Cost:** Free
- **Find it:** [instagram.com/saugavalleyrc](https://www.instagram.com/saugavalleyrc/)

## Brampton Benders Running Club
- **What:** Volunteer-led nonprofit bringing Brampton runners and walkers of all levels together for weekly group runs and community events
- **Where:** Brampton
- **Find it:** [bramptonbenders.com](https://www.bramptonbenders.com/)

## Kardia
- **What:** Riverside fitness community (formerly Tribe Fitness, founded 2013) with coached run programs and a big, welcoming run crew
- **Where:** Riverside
- **Find it:** [kardiaathletica.com](https://kardiaathletica.com/)

## Downsview parkrun
- **What:** Free, timed 5K every Saturday morning, run by volunteers. Walk, jog, run or cheer — register once before your first visit
- **Where:** Downsview Park, North York
- **When:** Saturdays 9am
- **Cost:** Free
- **Find it:** [parkrun.ca/downsview](https://www.parkrun.ca/downsview/)
