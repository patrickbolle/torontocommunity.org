# 🎧 Underground DJs & Dance Nights in Toronto

## Afrique Like Me
- **What:** Toronto collective Razaq El Toro, Martin Ses and Sonic Griot curate African electronic and underground dance music from across the diaspora. Their Eko Electronic nights mix Afrobeats, Afro house and alternative club sounds into a joyful, cross-generational dance floor
- **Where:** Toronto
- **Find it:** [afriquelikeme.com](https://www.afriquelikeme.com/)

## Box of Kittens
- **What:** A Toronto underground institution since the late 2000s. Mike Gibbs, Jamie Kidd, Hali and Fabio Palermo throw DIY warehouse and loft parties built on deep house and techno. Unpretentious, heads-down dancing and a crowd that knows the records
- **Where:** Toronto
- **Find it:** [@boxofkittensmusic](https://www.instagram.com/boxofkittensmusic/)

## Promise Cherry Beach
- **What:** A long-running Toronto summer ritual: Sunday afternoon electronic parties on the sand at Cherry Beach, with local and international DJs and a free-spirited, mixed crowd. One of the city's most beloved open-air dance floors
- **Where:** Cherry Beach
- **When:** Sundays, summer
- **Find it:** [promisecherrybeach.com](https://promisecherrybeach.com/)

## Yes Yes Y'all
- **What:** Toronto's long-running queer hip-hop and dancehall party, founded by Nino Brown, Sammy Rawal, Stunts, Yes Yes Jill and L Rock. A warm, wildly mixed crowd, classic club anthems and a dance floor that stays packed until close
- **Where:** Nest, 423 College Street, and Standard Time
- **Find it:** [yesyesyall.com](https://www.yesyesyall.com/)

## System Toronto
- **What:** A Toronto techno collective and rave crew behind the TTC subway rave and early Holy Priest shows. They pair hard, fast club sounds with a community-first outlook, the kind of party where you leave with new friends
- **Where:** Toronto
- **Find it:** [systemtoronto.com](https://www.systemtoronto.com/)

## Bodyclock
- **What:** A techno party series at The Piston with residents Zola, Miss Kleio and Mission Control, plus guest selectors. Queer-friendly, intimate and focused on the music rather than the spectacle
- **Where:** The Piston, 937 Bloor Street West
- **Find it:** [bodyclock.ca](https://www.bodyclock.ca/)

## Cherry Bomb
- **What:** Running since 2007, Cherry Bomb is Toronto's longest-running party for queer women, trans and non-binary people, a flirty, inclusive night of pop, house and club classics. Big Pride editions and a welcoming crowd of regulars
- **When:** Monthly, roving venue
- **Find it:** [@cherrybombtoronto](https://www.instagram.com/cherrybombtoronto/)

## Technoir Collective
- **What:** A Toronto DJ and event collective founded by Kika, Miss Kleio and Jackie Spade to spotlight DJs of colour and underrepresented artists. Safer, kinder dance floors with serious techno and house selectors
- **Where:** Toronto
- **Find it:** [@technoir_collective](https://www.instagram.com/technoir_collective/)

## Venues & Spaces

## CODA
- **What:** Toronto's basement temple for underground dance music, with a custom PK sound system and a steady lineup of techno and house heavyweights. Dark, loud and built for dancing
- **Where:** 794 Bathurst Street
- **Find it:** [codatoronto.com](https://codatoronto.com/)

## The Piston
- **What:** A snug Bloorcourt bar and live room that hosts techno, house and leftfield electronic nights, including Bodyclock, alongside live bands. Good sound and an easygoing crowd
- **Where:** 937 Bloor Street West
- **Find it:** [thepiston.ca](https://thepiston.ca/)

## Standard Time
- **What:** A second-floor Geary Avenue event space that has become a regular stop for underground electronic bookings, from touring acts to local crews. Industrial, no-frills and all about the sound system
- **Where:** 165 Geary Avenue
- **Find it:** [standardtime.to](https://standardtime.to/)
