# 🔮 Tarot / Astrology / Spiritual in Toronto

## Astrology Toronto
- **What:** A non-profit astrology organization that has served the city's astrological community since 1986. Its lecture season brings members together for talks, workshops, a lending library and an intergenerational mentorship program, with events in person and online
- **Where:** Tranzac Club, 292 Brunswick Ave, The Annex
- **Find it:** [astrologytoronto.com](https://www.astrologytoronto.com/)

## Toronto Tarot Meetup Group
- **What:** A 500-plus-member practice group for readers of every level, from first-deck beginners to veterans. Evenings are hands-on — compare decks and spreads, swap readings and talk symbolism in a welcoming, low-pressure room
- **Where:** Alternity, 333 Bloor St W
- **When:** Tuesday evenings
- **Find it:** [meetup.com/toronto-tarot-meetup-group](https://www.meetup.com/toronto-tarot-meetup-group/)

## The Rock Store
- **What:** A crystal healing centre on Harbord that doubles as a gathering place for Toronto's astrology crowd. Astrology classes, natal readings, Reiki and ritual workshops run alongside the shop, and owner Samantha teaches and sees clients there full time
- **Where:** 154 Harbord St
- **Find it:** [therockstore.ca](https://www.therockstore.ca/)

## The Hermit's Lamp
- **What:** A tarot shop, studio and community space in Leslieville run by reader, teacher and artist Andrew McGregor. It hosts beginner tarot classes, art shows, guest readers and a recurring queer tarot gathering — practical, friendly and open to newcomers
- **Where:** 276 Carlaw Ave, Unit 209B, Leslieville
- **Find it:** [thehermitslamp.com](https://thehermitslamp.com/)

## Springdale Church
- **What:** A Spiritualist church serving the Greater Toronto Area since 1939, with mediumship, spiritual healing and the Seven Principles at its centre. Sunday divine service is open to all, and the Thursday development circle is where regulars practice mediumship together
- **Where:** 30 Merritt Road
- **When:** Sundays 10:30am; Thursdays 7pm
- **Find it:** [springdalechurch.ca](https://www.springdalechurch.ca/)

## Happy Soul Crystals
- **What:** A Bloor West crystal shop and spiritual hub that runs its own in-store events — workshops, guided meditations and private group gatherings. A warm, browse-and-stay-awhile crowd of crystal and tarot regulars
- **Where:** 961 Bloor St W
- **Find it:** [happysoulcrystals.com](https://happysoulcrystals.com/)

## Venues & Resources

## Alternity
- **What:** A café and community hub at Bloor and Spadina that hosts tarot meetups, astrology-and-tarot socials and esoteric workshops. The kind of place where you can walk in for a coffee and leave in a conversation about your chart
- **Where:** 333 Bloor St W
- **Find it:** [alternity.ca](https://www.alternity.ca/)

## The Tranzac Club
- **What:** A member-run community arts club in the Annex, and the in-person home of Astrology Toronto's lecture series. A laid-back, volunteer-powered room that also hosts folk music, theatre and other community gatherings
- **Where:** 292 Brunswick Ave
- **Find it:** [tranzac.org](https://tranzac.org/)
