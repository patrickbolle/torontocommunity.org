# Anti-slop constraints — do not deviate without explicit approval

1. No cream, beige, tan, yellowed paper or dark-mode page backgrounds. Use `#FFFFFF`.
2. No maple leaf mark. The city name is the wordmark; optional city-specific tiny mark is secondary.
3. No category icons: no emoji, outline icon packs, generic SVG icons, Lucide category decoration, emoji-styled cards.
4. No fake vintage treatment: no tape, stamp marks, ripped paper, risograph distress, grain, photocopy artifacts.
5. No polished AI illustration treatment: no gradients, glows, soft outlines, painterly shading, ambient shadows, faux 3D bevels.
6. No pixel-font UI or large regions of monospace. Normal sans-serif for usability.
7. No six giant category tiles as the only directory entry point. Show subcategories and counts immediately.
8. No repeated three-column card matrix for every section. Use editorial asymmetry and typographic hierarchy.
9. No overly rounded controls, floating pills, decorative stars, random arrows, or giant iconography.
10. No copying the Toronto artwork into Vancouver and recolouring it. Use city-specific built scenes.
11. No fake local listings or invented inventory metrics. Bind to the actual database/content.
12. No speculative city symbolism. A giant CN Tower in every thumbnail is not local depth.
13. Do not ask the developer LLM to redraw the provided assets. Import them unchanged unless given a targeted art-change request.
